AAAI reference format

AAAI-27 papers use the AAAI-27 author kit. aaai2027.sty sets the bibliography style to aaai2027.bst for you, and the submission allows 7 pages of content plus up to 2 pages reserved for references.

Checked 2026-10-01 against the sources linked at the end of this page.

What AAAI expects

  • Reference style: Author-year through natbib, formatted by aaai2027.bst.
  • Style file: \usepackage[submission]{aaai2027} on \documentclass[letterpaper]{article} for the anonymous submission.
  • Length: The main submission PDF can have up to 9 pages, with pages 8 and 9 reserved for references. That leaves up to 7 pages of other content.
  • Paper: US Letter, two-column AAAI style, Type 1 or TrueType fonts.
  • Review: Double-blind. Remove author and affiliation information, and leave out acknowledgements.

Setting up the bibliography

The kit's instructions say the style file already sets the bibliography style, so you do not add a \bibliographystyle command. In the preamble and at the end of the document:

\usepackage[submission]{aaai2027}
\usepackage{natbib}
...
\bibliography{refs}

The template marks the natbib line “do not change this and do not add any options to it”. Keep aaai2027.bst next to your .tex file.

LaTeX template

The official kit is the AAAI-27 Author Kit, a zip with aaai2027.sty, aaai2027.bst, an anonymous submission template, a camera-ready template and the reproducibility checklist.

Common mistakes

  • Adding your own \bibliographystyle line or natbib options. The kit says not to.
  • Letting other content run onto page 8. Pages 8 and 9 are for references only.
  • Separating authors with commas in a hand-edited list. AAAI style uses semicolons, as the entries above show.
  • Leaving acknowledgements in the review version.

Frequently asked

What bibliography style does AAAI use?

aaai2027.bst for AAAI-27. The aaai2027.sty file sets it, so the document has no \bibliographystyle line.

Do references count toward the AAAI page limit?

They have their own allowance: the submission can be 9 pages, with pages 8 and 9 reserved for references and up to 7 pages for everything else.

Where is the AAAI LaTeX template?

In the AAAI-27 Author Kit, linked from the AAAI-27 submission instructions.
