Questions

What does “for life” mean for Vitae Plus?
The key you get is valid for 100 years and never asks to renew, and Plus stays unlocked for as long as Purplelink offers Vitae Plus. If Plus is ever discontinued, we will say so first, and the key keeps working in the version of Vitae you have.
Do I need the Suite to use Vitae?
No. Vitae is free, with all core record-keeping and no limits. Plus is the optional paid layer on top, and the Suite includes it for life.
Will I get future updates?
Yes. Every ModernTex and Outbound Veil update is included forever, including future major versions, and so is every Keyfeel update. Legroom and Tapefolio updates to the version you buy are included, and Vitae updates come as they are released.
Can I try them first?
Yes. ModernTex, Outbound Veil, Legroom, Keyfeel and Tapefolio each have a free 7-day trial, and Vitae is free. After you buy, each app takes its license key: paste it into the copy you already have.
What is the refund policy?
Email ben@purplelink.llc within 14 days of purchase and we will refund the full amount you paid to the original payment method, with no reason needed. A refunded Suite's ModernTex, Outbound Veil, Legroom, Keyfeel and Tapefolio license keys and its Vitae Plus key are no longer licensed for use.
Which Macs does it run on?
ModernTex, Outbound Veil and Legroom need macOS 14 or later, and Keyfeel needs macOS 13 or later, all on Apple silicon or Intel. Tapefolio needs macOS 26 or later and Apple silicon, with no Intel version. Vitae needs macOS 15 or later. There is no Windows or iPhone version.
Does Outbound Veil send my text anywhere?
No. The checking runs on your Mac, nothing you type is stored or sent, and the app has no analytics. Its page lists the apps it has been tested in.
Does Legroom send my files or measurements anywhere?
No. There is no account and no analytics. Apart from the daily update check that every copy makes and the Buy link, Legroom does not use the network.
Does Keyfeel read what I type?
No. It reads which key was pressed, as a key code, so it can choose a sound, and it does not read the characters. It works offline and needs the macOS Input Monitoring permission.
Does Tapefolio send my recordings anywhere?
No. Transcription, speaker labels, identifier removal and OCR run on your Mac. Only the optional model downloads inside the app and its check for a new version use the internet. It will mishear words and mislabel speakers, so check any text you quote against the recording.
