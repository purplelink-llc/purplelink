Budgetcast

A budgeting app for iPhone and Mac that keeps your transactions on your device and shows spending forecasts as ranges instead of single numbers.

Budgetcast is a working title and the app does not exist yet. This page is a way to find out whether enough people want it. Nothing below is a shipped feature, and the numbers are illustrations.

What you would not get

  • The first version would run on iPhone and Mac only. An Android app or a web version is not planned.
  • Bank sync coverage will vary by institution, and some banks may not connect. Manual entry and file import would cover the gaps.
  • Estimates and projections show what a set of assumptions implies. They are not financial, tax or investment advice.
  • There is no promised feature list, price or date, because the app has not been built.

Questions

Where would my data live?

The plan is local-first: your transactions are stored on your iPhone or Mac, and forecasts are computed there. Purplelink would not receive them. This is a design goal for an app that does not exist yet, not a description of shipped software.

Why a waitlist?

Building this is months of work. I would like to know whether enough people want it before I start, and which parts matter most. The two select fields on the form are there for that.

What will it cost?

The price is undecided. The $8 a month question on the form tests one possible price and is not an announcement of it.

What happens to my email?

It is stored with the form answers so I can count interest and write to you if the app gets built. I will not share it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
