Scholar Utility Belt Pro

Scholar Utility Belt is a free Chrome extension that adds journal rankings, author metrics and retraction warnings to Google Scholar. Pro is an optional tier on top of it for review, export and library work. It has a 14-day trial, and it costs $40 once, $3 a month or $20 a year. If you installed the extension before Pro existed, the Pro features that existed then are still free for you.

Add to Chrome Main product page

There is no checkout on this site. The trial and the purchase both start inside the extension, on its Options page.

What stays free

The reading and browsing aids are free for everyone, new installs included.

  • Journal quality badges from the packaged ranking lists, and a venue list of your own that you upload as a CSV.
  • The result action grid, citation velocity and the Emerging signal.
  • Retraction and tortured-phrase warnings, related works and the query trend tracker.
  • The saved-paper library with notes, tags, collections, and merging export and import.
  • Author summary metrics (h-index, g-index and the others) and author filters.

What Pro adds

Pro covers workflow tools that take a power user real time: systematic reviews, citation mapping, benchmarking an author against peers, and moving a library into other tools. "Older" means the feature existed when Pro shipped in version 0.6.0; those are the ones early installs keep. Everything else in the table needs the trial, a purchase or App Pass.

Trial, price and App Pass

  • 14-day trial. New installs can start it from the Pro section of the extension's Options page. Everyone, including early installs, can use it for the newer Pro features.
  • $40 once (lifetime), $3 a month or $20 a year. All three unlock the same features. Payment goes through ExtensionPay and Stripe, and ExtensionPay collects your email address.
  • App Pass. If you already subscribe to App Pass, which covers many browser extensions with one subscription, you can turn it on in Options to unlock Pro. It is off by default, and nothing contacts joinapppass.com until you switch it on.

If you installed before Pro

The seven Pro features that existed when Pro shipped stay free on your install, permanently: author compare, citation lineage, the extended bibliometrics (p-index, FWCI, Relative Citation Ratio and influential citations), narrative CV, the systematic-review workspace, the citation-graph overlay and the Publish-or-Perish report.

That promise covers those seven and nothing added since. The newer Pro features need the trial, a purchase or App Pass for everyone, early installs included. Removing the extension and installing it again counts as a fresh install.

Limits worth knowing

  • No institution has reviewed it. It is made by one person. No university, library or publisher has checked or endorsed it, and it is not part of any institutional license.
  • Ranking lists are copies. The lists ship inside the extension as snapshots. For anything that goes into a tenure file or a funding application, check the venue against the publisher of the list.
  • Signals from heuristics are hedged. Emerging, tortured-phrase warnings, claim-evidence chips and reproducibility signals are rules applied to titles and snippets. They can miss things and can flag harmless text. Read them as a prompt to look at the paper, not as a finding. The triage card and Ask my shortlist show a value only when it appears word for word in the saved text, which proves the words are there and not that they are the right ones.
  • AI is optional. The triage card uses Chrome's built-in model on your device, when your Chrome version and hardware support it. Ask my shortlist uses your own API key, and the provider you choose receives the text you confirm. Neither runs unless you turn it on.
  • Chrome only. It is installed from the Chrome Web Store.

What the extension sends, and to whom, is in the privacy policy. The extension has no Purplelink server and no analytics. The page you are reading is counted like any page on this site, with cookieless analytics.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scholar Utility Belt still free?
Yes. The core is free and open source under the ISC license: venue badges, the result action grid, citation velocity, the saved-paper library, author summary metrics, retraction and tortured-phrase warnings, the query trend tracker, related works and your own journal list. Pro is optional.
How do I start the 14-day trial?
Open the extension's Options page and use the Pro section. The trial and any purchase go through ExtensionPay, which asks for your email address. There is no checkout on purplelink.llc.
What happens when the trial ends?
The free features keep working. Pro features show a locked card in the place where the feature would be, with the option to buy.
I installed before Pro existed. Do I have to pay for anything?
Not for the seven Pro features that existed when Pro shipped in version 0.6.0: author compare, citation lineage, the extended bibliometrics, narrative CV, the systematic-review workspace, the citation-graph overlay and the Publish-or-Perish report. Those stay free on your install. Pro features added after that, which are the ones marked as needing a trial, purchase or App Pass in the table above, need a trial, a purchase or App Pass for everyone.
What counts as installed before Pro?
An install that was updated from a version before 0.6.0. The extension records once, when it is installed or updated, whether it was a fresh install, and never changes that record. Removing the extension and installing it again counts as a fresh install.
What does the $40 lifetime price cover?
It is a single payment with no renewal. The monthly plan is $3 and the yearly plan is $20, and both unlock the same features as the lifetime plan.
Can I use Pro on a second computer?
Yes. In the extension, choose Already purchased and sign in through ExtensionPay with the email you used. You do not need to pay again.
What is App Pass?
App Pass is a separate subscription, sold by joinapppass.com, that covers many browser extensions. If you already have one, you can turn it on in Options and it unlocks Pro. Nothing contacts joinapppass.com until you do.
Do the AI features send my papers anywhere?
Only if you turn them on. The triage card uses Chrome's built-in model on your device when your Chrome version and hardware support it. Ask my shortlist uses an API key you supply for Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini. It shows the provider, the number of papers, an estimated cost and the exact text before you press Send, and it sends nothing else.
Is there a license for a lab or a class?
Licenses are for one person. If several people in a lab or a course want Pro, write to ben@purplelink.llc.
Where is the source?
On GitHub at BenAmpel/ScholarUtilityBelt, under the ISC license, so you can read what the extension does before you install it.
