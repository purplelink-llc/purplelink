Installing

Open the disk image

Drag Legroom to Applications, then open it from there. The app is signed and notarized by Purplelink LLC and needs macOS 14 or later.

Find it in the menu bar

Legroom has no Dock icon and no window to start with. Look for its free-space readout in the menu bar and click it to open the window with Clean Now, Space Map, Find Files, Uninstaller, What Grew and Settings.

Full Disk Access is optional

Without it, Legroom skips the folders macOS protects, such as Desktop, Documents and Downloads, and says which ones it could not read. Space Map, Find Files and the Uninstaller explain how to turn it on if you want it.

The app checks for new versions once a day and asks before installing one. You can also check by hand from its menu.
