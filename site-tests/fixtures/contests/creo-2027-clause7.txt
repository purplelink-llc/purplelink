7. Copyright and usage
7.1 Each author keeps full ownership of the copyright in each Entry.
7.2 By entering, the author grants the Organiser and its Event Partners a non-exclusive, irrevocable licence to use any image submitted to the competition throughout the world for three years after the announcement in April 2027, in all media, for the promotion of the author and the awards, including but not limited to:
(a) judging the competition;
(b) exhibiting the winners and runners-up at exhibitions run by the Organiser;
(c) inclusion on the website and in a book, magazine or similar;
(d) promotional materials for the competition and exhibitions;
(e) competition and exhibition related products to be sold by the Organiser or a third party, following the individual agreement by the author;
(f) sub-licensing the entries to the press for reproduction in connection with the competition;
(g) letting viewers of the website and the awards app view and store images for personal, non-commercial use;
(h) inclusion in award galleries on Event Partner devices for three years after the announcement, after which an included image will remain perpetually in the device royalty free.
