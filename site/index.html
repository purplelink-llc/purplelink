Before you submit

Find what Reviewer 2 will find, first.

Paper Review puts your manuscript in front of four AI reviewers (a methodology critic, a statistical skeptic, a data integrity officer and an editor), checks every reference against CrossRef and scans the figures. You get a prioritized list of fixes, quoted from your own text, in minutes. No account; the manuscript is deleted when you retrieve the review.
