## Crossword clue rules (the weekly routine follows these)
A clue file is a JSON object `{"ANSWER": "clue", ...}` with one clue for every entry in the grid file of the same date. The validator (`clue_problems` in `scripts/gen_games_extra.py`) rejects a clue that is missing, over 110 characters, contains its answer (or most of it), or uses a dash character, emoji or exclamation mark.

- Original wording only. Never copy or lightly rephrase a clue from a published crossword.
- The clue must match the answer's part of speech, tense and number: a plural answer gets a plural clue, a past-tense answer a past-tense clue. Mark abbreviations ("Abbr.") and foreign words ("in Paris").
- No cross-references to other entries. Nothing political, sexual, graphic or about real private people. No brand promotion.
- Difficulty ladder by weekday (the grid already gets bigger and its words rarer):
  - Monday, Tuesday: plain definitions a newcomer can solve.
  - Wednesday, Thursday: shorter or lightly indirect clues; context phrases ("Pitcher's stat").
  - Friday, Saturday: vague or double-meaning clues; a trailing question mark marks light wordplay.
  - Sunday: Wednesday-level cleverness over a big grid; keep it fair.
- Plain ASCII punctuation (straight quotes are fine).

## Engagement features
- XP and level are derived from saved progress (`PLGames.xpOf`). The hub shows today's progress and a "next up" button.
- Streak saver: `recordResult` forgives one missed day per rolling week (logged in `stats.freezes`; merged by `netlify/lib/games-logic.mjs`).
- Confetti (`confetti.js`) skips users who prefer reduced motion.
- Optional streak reminder email: `netlify/functions/games-reminder.mjs` (scheduled 00:00 UTC), opt-in on /games/account/, one-click unsubscribe, at most one email a day, only when a streak of two or more days is alive.
- House promo: `site.js` places one random Purplelink app card above the footer on every page except checkout, receipt and account pages.
