Free Chrome extension

Scholar Utility Belt

Scholar Utility Belt is a free Chrome extension that adds journal quality badges, citation velocity, author metrics, lineage trees and retraction alerts to Google Scholar results. The ranking lists ship inside the extension, so nothing you search leaves your browser.

Add to Chrome, free Source on GitHub

About 1,000 researchers use it. Rated 4.5 out of 5 from 16 ratings on the Chrome Web Store.

What it adds

Context next to every result

Journal quality badges

ABDC, FT50, UTD24 and SJR rankings shown beside each result, so you can see where a paper appeared without looking the venue up.

Citation velocity

How quickly each paper is being cited, shown next to its total citation count.

Author metrics

h-index and g-index for authors, shown on their Google Scholar profiles.

Academic lineage

Academic lineage trees that place an author among their advisors and students.

Retraction alerts

A visible warning on results that have been retracted, before you cite them.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no remote lookups for rankings. MIT-licensed, so you can read exactly what it does.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scholar Utility Belt free?
Yes. It is free and MIT-licensed, with no paid tier.
Does it track what I search?
No. The journal ranking lists are bundled with the extension, so badges are looked up locally in your browser. There is no account and no analytics.
Which journal lists does it show?
ABDC, the Financial Times 50 (FT50), the UT Dallas 24 (UTD24) and SCImago Journal Rank (SJR).
Which browsers does it support?
Google Chrome, from the Chrome Web Store.
