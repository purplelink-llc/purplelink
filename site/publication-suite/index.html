Publication suite

Reviews for one paper, from submission to response letter

Three AI reviews follow a manuscript through peer review. Paper Review reads it before you submit. Revision Review checks the revised version against that first report. Response Review reads your response letter against the reviewers' comments, or drafts the letter for you. Each one is paid once, and you do not need an account.

Which review do I need?

Each manuscript goes to Anthropic's Claude API for the review and is not used for training. What happens to your manuscript

The three reviews

Each review runs in minutes and arrives on a page that updates itself, with a copy by email.

  1. Before you submit

    Paper Review

    $14, $24 or $39

    Seven AI reviewers read the manuscript the way a panel would (methods, statistics, data integrity, equations, the literature, the numbers and the editor's view). Every reference is checked against CrossRef and the figures are scanned. You get a Markdown report and an annotated PDF.

    • Standard, $14. One pass by the seven reviewers. 4 to 8 minutes.
    • Journal, $24. Standard plus a compliance check against the target journal (25 venues). Most authors pick this one.
    • Deep, $39. Two passes. In the second, each reviewer reads the others' findings and sharpens its own. The journal check is included. 10 to 15 minutes.
    Start a Paper Review

  2. After the reviews come back

    Revision Review

    $5

    Upload the revised manuscript with the Markdown report from your first Paper Review. Revision Review goes through each finding in that report and marks whether the revision addressed it.

    Check a revision

  3. Before you resubmit

    Response Review

    $12 or $24

    Three AI reviewers read the reviewer comments, your response letter and the revised manuscript. They flag replies that are missing or that do not answer the point, and estimate whether the editor will accept or ask for more.

    • Check a letter, $12. Upload the revised PDF, then paste the comments and your letter.
    • Draft and check, $24. Upload the revised PDF and paste the comments. You get a reply to every comment, checked against the revised PDF and then reviewed. You edit it before sending.
    Check or draft a letter

Which one to buy

Start from where the paper is now.

You are about to submit
Buy a Paper Review. The $24 Journal tier is the usual choice, because it also checks the manuscript against the journal's own rules. Pick Deep ($39) for a paper you expect a hard panel on.
You have a revise-and-resubmit
If you ran a Paper Review on the first version, a Revision Review ($5) tells you which findings the revision still leaves open. Then check your response letter ($12), or have one drafted and checked ($24).
You want one paper covered start to finish
Buy the Publication Pack ($59). It holds the Deep Review, the Revision Review and the response letter draft, and costs $9 less than buying the three one at a time.
You run a lab or a group
Buy a pack of 5 or 20 reviews for a few papers a year, or a lab plan for a group that submits every month.

What happens to your manuscript

  • The manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for the review. Anthropic keeps API inputs for up to 30 days and does not train on them.
  • The report is emailed to you as a PDF. The manuscript and the online copy of the report are deleted within about a day after you first open the result, or within 48 hours if you never open it.
  • If a review was not useful, email ben@purplelink.llc within 14 days of purchase and I will refund it.
