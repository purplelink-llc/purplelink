Data handling

Where a manuscript goes

For a research office or ethics board, in brief. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Paper Review and the other paid checks
The manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, inputs are retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and are not used for model training. Reference titles and DOIs are sent to CrossRef. Purplelink deletes the manuscript and the result when the result is retrieved.
Clinical and patient data
Do not upload identifiable patient data. Redact it before upload.
The free tools
Files are processed in memory to produce the result and are not stored.

Desktop apps. Your documents and records stay on your Mac.
