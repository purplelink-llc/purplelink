# Photo Licensing — Platform Matrix

Researched 2026-08-04 (web-verified; this space moved a lot in 2025–26).
Strategy: **maximum breadth, non-exclusive everywhere**, with a partitioned
premium layer. See `README.md` for strategy, `launch-runbook.md` for sequencing.

## 2025–26 market events that invalidate older guides

- **Getty–Shutterstock merger terminated** (July 2026, UK CMA divestiture demand).
  They remain separate companies — maintain separate accounts.
- **EyeEm shut down** Jan 2026 (Freepik acquired). **Twenty20** died 2022.
- **Alamy commission cut effective 2026-09-01**: Bronze 15% (<$250/yr), Silver 20%
  ($250–$3k), Gold 40% ($3k+). Payout min rises to $75.
- **Pond5 photos cut to 30%** (Jan 2025). **Canva contributors still paused** to
  new applicants. **Freepik rebranded "Magnific"** (Apr 2026), program unchanged.
- **Earnings gravity moved to Adobe Stock** — tracked portfolios report Adobe at
  ~2–4x Shutterstock revenue for identical files (backyardsilver.com 2025/2026).
- Industry RPD fell ~12% in 2025 under AI-content pressure. Breadth + consistent
  uploads still produce real side income; Shutterstock-first strategies are over.

## Print-on-demand (product sales, not licensing — non-exclusive, stacks on everything)

| Channel | Your take | Verdict |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Fine Art America / Pixels** | You set markup, keep 100% of it (metal 16×20 base $60.30) | **Start here.** Photography-native buyers; free 25-image tier, ~$30/yr unlimited; monthly payouts, no floor; US W-9/1099 |

| **Redbubble** | Tiered account fees since 2025 + surcharge on markup >20% | Only after FAA validates |
| **Society6** | Fixed 5–10% of net since the 2025 curated pivot | Skip |

### Why Displate is deferred (researched 2026-08-05)

Open to all, free, fully non-exclusive (ToU §3.1 — artist keeps all rights), so there's
no contractual reason to avoid it. The reasons to skip it anyway are structural:

- **Photographers report near-zero returns there.** Public first-person numbers from
  photographers: $6.47 lifetime (2 sold) and $84.75 lifetime (26 sold). The platform's
  own stats — $30M paid across 42,000 artists — average ~$700 *lifetime* per artist.
- **The audience is fandom, not landscape.** Displate's own nav order: Anime → Gaming →
  Movies → Cars → Nature. Licensed Marvel/Star Wars/Warhammer shops dominate.
- **Displate Customs (launched Sept 2025)** lets buyers upload and print their own
  photos — cannibalizing exactly the "nice landscape on metal" purchase.
- **You don't control price.** Fixed per-size fee, and ToU §3.7(c) has you consent to
  discounts that cut your fee proportionally. A $4.50 M-size sale pays $3.60 at 20% off,
  $2.70 at 40%. (Displate settled a US class action over synthetic "list" prices —
  *Yates v. GWD Concept*, ~$2.77M.)
- **Real per-image labor:** forced 1.4:1 crop (Nikon native 3:2 doesn't fit), **no IPTC
  metadata ingestion** — every field typed by hand, and tags are locked after upload —
  plus an artist-reported 1-upload/day throttle.
- **Payout friction:** $50 floor, PayPal only, 10-day hold plus up to 45 days.
- **Tax:** Polish company (GWD Concept Sp. z o.o.), no W-9/1099, withholding reserved
  but unpublished (ToU §3.12).

**The one condition that flips this:** Share & Earn pays **41% of net** on sales arriving
through your own affiliate link (~$22.55 vs $4.50 on an M poster). If Purplelink ever has
real audience traffic to drive, Displate becomes a good fulfillment partner rather than a
marketplace. Given current site traffic is near zero, that's a later-stage revisit.

## Skip list (with reasons)

- **Wirestock** — 15% aggregator cut, files live under their accounts (no
  migration of sales rank), post-exit selling window ≈ months-to-a-year. Direct
  accounts + Xpiks is strictly better for a technical operator.
- **500px Licensing** — 25% of opaque Getty/VCG net, 1.3/5 Trustpilot, duplicate-
  listing conflicts with a direct Getty port.
- **Society6** (2025 curated pivot, 5–10% fixed), **Redbubble** (2025 tiered
  account fees) — POD economics gutted.
- **EyeEm, Twenty20, Bigstock, Fotolia** — dead/absorbed.
- **Alamy Platinum tier** — 50% requires exclusivity; breadth is worth more.

## Exclusivity traps (checklist at every signup)

1. iStock/Getty exclusivity = per-contributor for stills. Sign **non-exclusive** ASA only.
2. Stocksy exclusivity covers **similars from the same shoot** — judged case-by-case.
3. Never set Alamy/Dreamstime per-image "exclusive" flags on multi-platform files.
4. 500px exclusive flag + a direct Getty port = contract breach.
5. Wirestock + own account on the same agency = duplicate-content flags.
6. Keep the commercial set out of free pools (Vecteezy Free, Freepik free).
7. Arcangel license model (RM/RM-Excl/RF) is permanent once chosen per image.

## Cross-platform workflow

Embed IPTC once (`scripts/photo-metadata.sh embed`) — Adobe, SSTK, Alamy,
Dreamstime, Depositphotos, 123RF, Pond5 all auto-read it on FTP ingest.
**Xpiks** (free, macOS) pushes one batch to all portals over FTP.
StockSubmitter is Windows-only now.
