Lenscrib

Lenscrib is for stock photo contributors. It drafts a title, a description and keywords for each photo on your Mac, fits them to each agency's rules, and writes them into your image files. Your photos are not uploaded.

How it works

You add your photos

Open a folder of images. Lenscrib reads each photo's GPS position and date and names the place and the date from an offline list, then a vision model on your Mac looks at the picture.

You read and fix each field

Every photo gets a draft title, description and keywords. You edit them, pick the agency to fit them to, and check the editorial caption line before anything is saved.

It writes the files and the CSV

Lenscrib writes the metadata into the image files and exports the CSV each agency asks for. Then you upload on the agency's site.

What it does

Title, description, keywordsA vision model that runs on your Mac looks at each photo and drafts all three. It is downloaded once from Hugging Face, about 3 GB, the first time you use it. After that it needs no connection.
Place and dateReads the photo's GPS position and date, and names the place and the date from an offline place list. No network is used for this step, so a location is never sent anywhere.
Keyword cleaningLowercases keywords, removes duplicates and drops generic words that do not help a buyer find the photo. The same cleaning is in the free keyword cleaner.
Agency rulesFits the metadata to the rules of Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Alamy, Vecteezy and Dreamstime.
Editorial captionsBuilds the editorial caption line from the place and the date, for the agencies and photos that need one.
SavingWrites the title, description and keywords into the image files as IPTC and XMP, and exports each agency's CSV.

What it will not do

Get every field rightThe model can be wrong. It guesses species and places, and it can misread what a photo shows. You review every field before saving. Check any species, place or event named in a title or an editorial caption before you submit it.
Upload to agenciesLenscrib does not log in to Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Alamy, Vecteezy or Dreamstime and does not upload for you. It prepares the files and the CSV.
Pass an agency's reviewFitting to an agency's rules keeps the text within its limits. It does not decide whether an agency accepts a photo.
Run on an Intel Mac or an older macOSIt needs macOS 14 or later and Apple silicon. There is no Windows, iPhone or iPad version.
Send your photos anywhereThere is no cloud description service and there are no accounts. Nothing you open in Lenscrib is uploaded.

Privacy and what downloads

Your photos stay on your Mac

The description model runs on your Mac and the place comes from the photo's own GPS with an offline list. There are no accounts.

One model download

The vision model, about 3 GB, downloads once from Hugging Face the first time you use it. The only other use of the internet is the app's check for a new version, which carries the app's name and version and nothing from your photos. Details are on the privacy page.

You decide what is saved

Nothing is written into a file until you have reviewed the photo and saved it.

Frequently asked questions

What does Lenscrib need to run?
macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. There is no Intel version.
Do my photos leave my Mac?
No. The model that writes the descriptions runs on the Mac, and the place and date come from the photo's own GPS and date with an offline place list, so no photo and no location is sent anywhere. The internet is used to download the vision model from Hugging Face once, about 3 GB, the first time you use it, and for the app's check for a new version. There is no account.
Can the model be wrong?
Yes. It guesses species and places, and it can misread a scene. Lenscrib shows every field for you to edit, and you review each photo before it saves anything. Do not submit a caption that states a species or a place you have not checked.
Does it upload to the agencies?
No. Lenscrib writes the metadata into your image files and exports a CSV in the format each agency asks for. You upload on the agency's own site.
Which agencies does it fit keywords to?
Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Alamy, Vecteezy and Dreamstime. Each has its own limits on title length, description length and number of keywords, and Lenscrib trims to the one you pick.
Does one license work on more than one Mac?
One license key works on two of your Macs. Paste the same key into Lenscrib on each one.
What happens when the trial ends?
The trial is the complete app for seven days from the first time you open it, with no account. To keep using Lenscrib after that, buy it. You get a license key by email and on the page after you pay; choose Enter License Key in Lenscrib and paste it. It works offline and needs no second download.
How do I get help?
Email ben@purplelink.llc. If you lose your download link, the Find a purchase page emails it again to the address you paid with.
Can I get a refund?
Email ben@purplelink.llc within 14 days of purchase. See the terms.
