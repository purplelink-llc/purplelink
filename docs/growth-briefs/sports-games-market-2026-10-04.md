# Sports games: who else is out there (2026-10-04)

Method: web searches and page fetches on 2026-10-04. Several competitor pages (Roster Relay, HOOPLINK, Sports Link) returned little detail, and almost none publish traffic, so everything below is what the pages say about themselves. Only Immaculate Grid has public numbers (100k to 200k plays a day in 2023).

## Verdict

All three formats already exist, and each has several free competitors. We are not first in any of them. The openings are narrower than "sports dles": era range, four leagues in one place, bidirectional chains, budget drafting, and credible ratings.

## Teamlink (teammate chain)

| Competitor | Sports | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Roster Relay (Hindsight Games, independent) | NBA, NFL, MLB | Daily, free wrong guesses, an "assist" list of everyone who played with your current name, a fame-weighted rarity measure. Closest rival. |
| Teammate Chain (austinkrance.com) | NBA, MLB, NFL, NHL | Rosters from 1976 on only, three strikes, tap cards for team history. |
| HOOPLINK, Dribble, Sports Link, Around the Horn (baseball) | NBA mostly | Dribble caps chains at 6; has Unlimited, Speed mode, accounts, a Griddy mode, donations only. |
| Link Up, Daily XI "Six Degrees" | Football (soccer) | Club teammates. |

Where we differ: any era including before 1976 (Russell to Brown), four leagues on a rotating daily sport, chains built from both ends, par from the true shortest path, a percentile against everyone who finished. Weak spot until the all-players database ships: our pool is the famous list only, while Roster Relay accepts every player.

Ideas that none of them seem to have: after the game, show other shortest chains; a "bridge" badge for players who connect eras; a puzzle type that forces an era jump (1960s to 2020s); a weekly "marathon".

## Gridlink (3x3 grid)

Immaculate Grid (Sports Reference, bought 2023) owns this space: MLB, NBA, WNBA, NFL, NHL and soccer, rarity from other players' answers, and a TV show deal. Followers: HoopGrids, Crossover Grid (harder award clues), Airball Hoop Grid, Puckdoku, Gridzy, Sportsdle grids, Dribble's Griddy.

Where we are behind: our clues are only team, decade, position and career length, and our rarity score is a fame tier rather than real crowd data. Others use awards, stats and draft clues.

Where we could be different: a "teammate of [star]" clue (ties to Teamlink and nobody does it as far as found), awards and draft clues from our data, crowd rarity once we have traffic. Head-on against Immaculate Grid is a poor bet, so keep Gridlink cheap.

## Unbeaten (perfect-season builder)

The most crowded of the three. The "82-0 game" is a viral genre with many clones: 82-0.com, 84-0.com, 82-0game.org, perfect-season.com, X-0 Games, True 82, Franchise Lab, and two serious multi-sport products.

- Sportsdle: 82-0 (draft from real team-seasons since 1979-80, three free seasons a day, Pro tier removes the limit and ads), 17-0 and 84-0. Ratings hidden until the end, from Box Plus/Minus and awards; odds shown (about 1 in 1,300 for casual drafts up to single-digit percent for well-built teams).
- Go Unbeaten (gounbeaten.com, apps): NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB and soccer. Random spins give a team-season to pick from, one daily challenge, a leaderboard, streaks.

Ours differs: budget-based picks from any era instead of a random spin, a chemistry bonus from real teammates, a weekday rule, odds shown. Weak spot: our ratings are our own tiers, not stats, so a basketball fan will see odd results.

## What to do

1. Make the ratings defensible. Derive them from public career numbers (peak seasons, awards) instead of tiers, and publish how. This is the main credibility gap in Unbeaten.
2. Teamlink is the best fit: finish the all-players database, then lean on era range and four leagues.
3. Add awards and draft clues to Gridlink and a "teammate of" clue.
4. Check names before heavy promotion. TeamLinkt is a sports team-management app with a trademark-like name, close to "Teamlink" (different field, but real). "Go Unbeaten" is a multi-sport 82-0 game, close to "Unbeaten". No game called Gridlink turned up. This is a search, not a trademark clearance.
5. Distribution: the searches people use are "Immaculate Grid alternative", "NBA teammate game", "82-0 game". Pages that answer those directly are the way in. Daily puzzles with no third-party ads is a real selling point, since Sportsdle gates play behind a Pro tier.
