Atlanta

Atlanta is home. The Midtown skyline from Piedmont Park at golden hour, the same skyline in a snowfall, the downtown connector from above, Georgia Tech at dusk, the Botanical Garden in bloom, and, below, the week in July 2025 when the MLB All-Star Game came to Truist Park.

Truist Park hosted the 2025 MLB All-Star Game on July 15, 2025, the first time Atlanta had the game since 2000.
