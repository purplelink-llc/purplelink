Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, "One Night Short: Sleep Restriction and Defect Detection in Code Review," for publication in Empirical Software Engineering.

Our central finding is that a single night of restricted sleep was associated with a measurable drop in how many seeded defects experienced developers caught during code review.

We believe the work fits the journal's focus on empirical studies of software engineering practice. Code review is a routine quality gate in most teams, yet the conditions under which reviewers work are rarely measured. The manuscript may interest readers working on reviewer performance, human factors, and the design of review workflows.

In a within-subjects experiment, 42 professional developers reviewed matched pull requests once after a normal night of sleep and once after a night restricted to four hours, in counterbalanced order. Each change contained seeded defects of known type. Mean detection fell from 61% to 48% under restriction, with the largest drop for logic errors, while the number of false-positive comments did not change. The sample came from a single organization, and the effect may differ for chronic sleep loss; we discuss both limitations.

This manuscript has not been published previously and is not under review elsewhere.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding Author Name]

FAQ

Why no PDF upload?
Cover letters are 95% built from the abstract and the target journal's scope. Skipping the full manuscript means we send much less of your unpublished IP to a third-party AI service. The trade-off: the letter cites the manuscript only at the level of detail your abstract supports.
Can you target a specific reviewer or editor?
You can paste an "author note" with that context when you compose. The model will weave it into the letter if it fits naturally.
What model produces the letter?
Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 with temperature 0.1. The same model + settings that power our main Paper Review tool.
What does Purplelink retain?
Nothing after delivery. The letter is deleted from our servers the moment you retrieve it. Your abstract and journal name are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which retains inputs for 30 days for abuse monitoring, never for training.
