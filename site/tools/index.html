Privacy-respecting tools, free and paid

The free tools are LaTeX, citation and conversion utilities for academic authors. They compile to PDF, convert to and from Word, diff versions, validate BibTeX, render equations and more. Your files sit in a temporary folder on our server only while the result is built, then they are deleted, so unpublished manuscripts are not kept. Tools marked In your browser do their work on your computer and upload nothing.

The paid Paper Review tool runs an AI red-team review of an uploaded manuscript. That review depends on a third-party language model (Anthropic’s Claude API), so its data-handling guarantees differ from the in-house free tools. The Paper Review page lists the retention details before you upload.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools free?
The LaTeX and citation utilities are free with no account and no install. The only rate limit is a daily fair-use cap. The Paper Review tool is paid (from $9 per review).
Do you store my files?
For the free tools: no. A file sits in a temporary folder on our server only while the result is built, then it is deleted. File contents are not logged. For the paid Paper Review tool, your manuscript is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API, which retains inputs for 30 days for abuse monitoring (not for training). Purplelink itself does not retain the manuscript or the resulting review after delivery. See the Paper Review page for full details.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Every tool runs in your browser, some against a hosted backend. No LaTeX installation, account or sign-up is needed.
