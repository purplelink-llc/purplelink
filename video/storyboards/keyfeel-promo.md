# Keyfeel promo: storyboard

Target: about 30 seconds including the Purplelink bumpers (product cut about 26 s). 16:9 for the site and YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. An 8 second silent hero loop and a poster for the Keyfeel page. Facts come only from `site/keyfeel/index.html`, `site/llms.txt` and `docs/products/keyfeel.md`. No statistics, ratings, testimonials or user counts.

Footage: real captures of the running app (version 1.2.0), taken with `screencapture`: each Settings tab as a window capture, and the menu-bar dropdown as a region capture of the menu plus the Keyfeel icon, set on the bar's own colour (neighbouring menu-bar icons removed). Nothing is drawn or generated. Sound: the app's own CC0 recordings (tactile, Cherry MX Brown, dome, mouse, brush scroll), mixed into a short bed and ducked under the narration. Voice: en-GB-SoniaNeural, as the Legroom and Outbound Veil promos.

## 0. Title card (about 1.2 s)
- **Sees:** app icon, "Keyfeel", "Real keyboard-switch sounds as you type."
- **Hears:** a few real tactile key presses start under the title.
- **Connects to next:** the menu window rises in.

## 1. Menu bar (about 3.5 s)
- **Sees:** the menu-bar icon and the open dropdown. A ring on the icon, then on "Keyboard sounds".
- **Understands:** it is a menu-bar app with switches for keyboard sounds, click sounds, ripple, haptics, headphones and microphone.
- **On-screen copy:** "1 Menu bar / Runs in the menu bar."
- **Narration:** "Keyfeel plays real switch sounds as you type."
- **Connects to next:** crossfade to Settings, Sounds tab.

## 2. Profiles (about 3.5 s)
- **Sees:** Settings, Sounds. A ring moves down the rows Tactile, Cherry MX Brown, Dome as each is named.
- **Hears:** a short run of keys from each recorded profile, on its name.
- **On-screen copy:** "2 Profiles / Recorded Tactile, Cherry MX Brown and Dome."
- **Narration:** "Tactile, Cherry MX Brown, or Dome."

## 3. Mouse, scroll, haptics (about 4.2 s)
- **Sees:** Settings, Input: ring on the click-sound rows, then the scroll-ticks row. A quick cut to the Feel tab with a ring on the trackpad haptics card.
- **Hears:** two mouse clicks, then a scroll flick that slows down. No sound for haptics (they are felt).
- **On-screen copy:** "3 Mouse and scroll / Click sounds and scroll ticks." then "4 Trackpad / Optional haptics on scroll and click."
- **Narration:** "Clicks, scrolling, and optional trackpad haptics."

## 4. Quiet (about 4.1 s)
- **Sees:** Settings, Apps: ring on "Silence Keyfeel in these apps", then on "Stay silent while any app is using the microphone".
- **Hears:** typing that stops as the voice says "silent"; the bed stays quiet after.
- **On-screen copy:** "5 Quiet / Silent in chosen apps and during mic use."
- **Narration:** "Silent in apps you choose, and while the microphone is on."

## 5. Privacy (about 4.4 s)
- **Sees:** the Permission card from Settings, General: ring on "Input Monitoring is allowed", then on the sentence about key codes and nothing leaving the Mac.
- **On-screen copy:** "6 Privacy / Key codes, not characters. Works offline."
- **Narration:** "It reads key codes, not characters, and works offline."

## 6. End card (about 4.5 s, settles)
- **Sees:** icon, "Keyfeel", pill "$9.99 once. 7-day free trial.", "purplelink.llc/keyfeel", then the Purplelink bumper.
- **Narration:** "Nine ninety-nine, once, with a seven day free trial."
- **Ends:** the bumper closes with the product URL.
