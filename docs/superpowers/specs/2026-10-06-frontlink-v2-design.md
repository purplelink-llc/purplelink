# Frontlink v2: design

Frontlink is a daily, deterministic, no-luck tactics battle in the spirit of Advance Wars and Wargroove, kept to a few
minutes. v1 shipped as a bare 7 by 6 grid with three units and one win rule, and an interface that was hard to read.
v2 keeps the "same battle for everyone, no randomness, replay-able from a move list" contract and adds the things that made
Advance Wars worth playing: an economy and production, units that counter each other, a commander with a power, varied
objectives, a ranking that rewards more than speed, fog of war on one day, and an interface that tells the player what is
possible and what will happen before they commit.

## Research notes (what the genre does well)

- Advance Wars: properties (cities, factories, HQ) give income and are captured by infantry (20 capture points, reduced by
  the unit's HP each turn); units are bought at factories with funds; every unit has a counter; terrain gives defense
  stars; commanders (COs) have a passive and a charged power; fog of war makes vision matter; missions are ranked on
  speed, power and technique, which pull against each other so there is no single best play.
- Wargroove and Advance Wars interfaces: a cursor over a grid, move range highlighted, attack range highlighted
  separately, a damage preview (damage dealt and taken) before an attack is confirmed, a small action menu next to the unit
  with the most useful action first, unit and terrain info always visible, and generous undo for a move that has not been
  committed.
- Clones and puzzle-tactics games (Picowars, Tiny Tactics, Puzzle Tactics): small maps, few units, one clear objective, and
  a threat or danger view so players can see where enemies can hit.

## Contract with the rest of the site (do not break)

- Deterministic: the map, armies, objective and enemy behaviour come from the day index
  (`PLGames.dayIndex(new Date(), "2026-10-04")`) and a seeded PRNG. No `Math.random`, no clock in the engine.
- The saved state is `today = {idx, moves, done}` plus a `v: 2` marker; `moves` replays through the engine. A save that
  does not replay, or has no `v: 2`, is dropped (v1 saves must not crash the page).
- Result: `won` (bool) and `tries` = days used (1 to 6) for `PLGames.recordResult`. The game calls
  `PLAch.check({game:"frontlink", idx, won, days, clean, rank})`, where `clean` means a win with no units lost.
- Share text (read by `site/games/share.js`, which turns it into a preview card; keep this exact shape):
  line 1 `Frontlink <idx+1> <days>/6` (or `X/6`), a blank line, one row of squares, one per day played (`■` destroyed an enemy
  unit that day, `▣` damaged one, `□` nothing), then a detail line `Rank <S|A|B|C|D>, units lost <n> of <m>`, then a blank
  line and `https://purplelink.llc/games/frontlink/`.
- `site/games/share.js`, `core.js` and the server lists are unchanged. The page script list is generated by
  `scripts/gen_games_pages.py`; a new script file `site/games/frontlink-engine.js` loads before `frontlink.js`.
- Hard site rules: strict CSP (external CSS and JS only, no inline styles), no emojis, plain calm copy with no buzzwords or
  em-dash tics, dark default with `[data-theme="light"]`, WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable, `prefers-reduced-motion`
  honoured, no third-party loads.

## Board and terrain

- 8 columns by 6 rows (48 tiles), seeded, point-symmetric enough to be fair. The player starts bottom left, the enemy top
  right. Every day must be winnable (see the tests).
- Terrain (move cost for foot / wheels / tracks, cover stars): plains 1/1/1, 0 stars; road 1/1/1, 0; forest 1/2/2, 1 star;
  mountain 2/impassable/impassable, 2 stars (infantry and bazooka only); water impassable; bridge (road over water) 1/1/1.
- Properties (all give 1 cover star): HQ (one per side), cities (neutral at the start, 2 to 4 per map, not on the HQ line),
  and factories (one per side, buys units). A unit standing on a friendly property heals 2 HP at the start of its side's
  day (cities and HQ; factories too).
- Capture: infantry and bazooka can capture. A capture needs 20 points; each day the unit stays and does not move, it
  subtracts its HP (10 HP = 10 points, so a full-HP unit needs 2 days). Damage to the capturer lowers progress next day.
  Leaving the tile resets progress. A captured city gives +100 funds a day to its owner from the next day.

## Economy and production

- Funds: each side starts with a set amount (default 300) and gains +100 per owned city per day, plus +100 base. Funds
  are shown at all times.
- Build: select an owned, empty factory to open the build menu; buy one unit per factory per day. A new unit appears on
  the factory tile and cannot act until the next day. Costs: Infantry 100, Bazooka 150, Scout 150, Tank 300, Artillery 300.
- The enemy builds too (same costs), following its own deterministic rules (see AI). This makes army composition visible
  and counter-able, which is the core decision.

## Units (10 HP each)

| Unit | Move type | Move | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry | foot | 3 | 1 | Captures. Cheap. |
| Bazooka | foot | 2 | 1 | Captures. Very strong against tanks. Weak against everything else. |
| Scout | wheels | 5 | 1 | Fast flanker, strong against infantry and artillery, weak against tanks. Cannot capture. |
| Tank | tracks | 4 | 1 | Strong all-round, weak against bazooka and artillery. |
| Artillery | tracks | 2 | 2 to 3 | Cannot move and fire the same day. Never counterattacks at range 1. |

Tune the 5 by 5 base-damage table (percent, applied as in the formula below) so that: Tank beats Infantry, Scout and
Artillery; Bazooka beats Tank; Infantry beats Bazooka and Artillery in a close fight and is the best capturer per
funds; Scout beats Infantry and Artillery; Artillery hurts everything at range and loses to anything that reaches it.
Keep every matchup's best case under 100 percent so no unit one-shots a full-HP unit.

Damage (as in v1, whole HP): `round(base[att][def] / 10 * attackerHP * (1 - 0.1 * stars * defenderHP / 10))` in HP
units, where `base` is in tenths of a unit (a 55 percent hit is 5.5), clamped to the defender's HP; a surviving defender
counterattacks with its remaining HP and its own base damage if the attacker is inside its range (artillery has a minimum
range of 2 so it never counters at range 1). Show both numbers before the attack is confirmed.

## Commanders (choose one before day 1; same three every day, one power use per battle)

- Ace: passive, all units move +1. Power "Blitz" (day 2 or later): every unit of yours that has not acted gets +2 move
  and may act again after moving, for this day only.
- Brick: passive, all units take 15 percent less damage. Power "Fortify": all your units count as having +2 cover stars
  until your next day starts.
- Sparks: passive, all units deal 10 percent more damage. Power "Overdrive": your units deal 30 percent more damage and
  your counterattacks do too, until your next day starts.
The commander is part of the saved moves (`["co", name]` first). The enemy has a fixed commander per day (no power).

## Objectives (one per weekday; shown in a banner that always stays on screen with live progress)

0 Sunday: Fog of war rout (destroy every enemy unit; vision radius 3 tiles from your units and properties, enemy units
  outside vision are hidden and shown as "?" markers where last seen).
1 Monday: Rout (destroy every enemy unit). Both sides start with a factory.
2 Tuesday: Take the HQ (infantry or bazooka ends a day on the enemy HQ with 20 capture points accumulated).
3 Wednesday: Capture the enemy factory (the enemy keeps building until you do).
4 Thursday: Hold the line (survive to the end of day 6 with your HQ; the enemy sends a deterministic wave on days 2 and
  4; win rank is by losses and kills because it always takes 6 days).
5 Friday: Convoy (your marked Tank must reach the exit tile on the enemy edge alive; the other units are escorts).
6 Saturday: Commander hunt (destroy the enemy "Commander" unit, a tank with 12 HP on or near their HQ, escorted; other
  enemy units do not need to die).
In every mode: you lose if your HQ is captured, all your units are dead and you cannot afford a unit, or the sixth day
ends without the objective met. Day numbers are 1 to 6 and each day is: your phase, then the enemy's phase.

## Ranking

Win rank S/A/B/C/D from three criteria that pull against each other: speed (days used), power (units lost) and
technique (funds left unspent counts for you, enemy units destroyed counts for you). A simple rule is fine, for example
points = 4 for finishing within par days (par from the winning-line search in the tests), 2 within par + 1, 0 otherwise;
+3 no units lost, +2 one, +1 two; +1 if you destroyed 4 or more enemy units or held 2 or more extra cities. S = 7 or more,
A = 5 to 6, B = 3 to 4, C = 1 to 2, D = a win that scores 0. A loss has no rank (`X`). Document the rule in the FAQ
text the lead will write.

## Enemy AI (deterministic, simple, readable)

Fixed unit order. Each enemy unit: if it can attack, choose the target that maximises (damage dealt value minus damage
taken value) with a tie-break by position, moving to the tile with the best cover that reaches it; otherwise move toward
the objective of its role (infantry and bazooka toward the nearest uncaptured city or your HQ; scouts toward your weakest
unit; tanks toward your nearest unit; artillery hold behind the front and shoot whatever is in 2 to 3 range after moving
only if it did not need to). The enemy factory builds the counter to your most common unit type when funds allow,
otherwise the cheapest unit, at most one a day. No randomness; the same position always gives the same enemy day.

## Interface requirements (the part v1 got wrong)

The board is isometric (the generated art is isometric): tiles are diamonds, units stand on them, taller things overlap
those behind. Keep real, keyboard-reachable buttons for every tile (clip-path diamond hit areas over the art), but a
single pointer handler may do hit-testing from the pointer position for accuracy. Arrow keys must follow the screen
directions (up-left, up-right and so on), not raw row and column axes, with a clear mapping explained in the on-screen
help.

- One cursor ring always shows the focused tile. A side panel (below the board on narrow screens) shows two cards: the
  unit under the cursor (name, HP bar and number, move, range, what it is strong and weak against, whether it has acted)
  and the terrain under the cursor (name, cover stars, move costs, owner and capture progress for properties).
- Select a unit: its reachable tiles get a blue tint, tiles it could attack get a red hatch, and a "threat" toggle (key T)
  shows every tile any enemy could hit next phase.
- Choose a destination: a small action menu appears beside the unit with the available actions (Attack, Capture, Wait,
  Cancel), most useful first. Choosing Attack lets you cycle targets; focusing a target shows a damage preview in an
  anchored tooltip: "Deal 6, take 2" with before and after HP bars. Enter confirms. Escape or Cancel backs out one step.
- Undo: since nothing is random, give "Undo" (key Z, undoes one action) and "Restart day" (key R) with no penalty. The
  saved move list always matches what is on screen.
- "End day" (key E): if some units have not acted, an inline confirmation says how many and offers "End day anyway" or
  "Keep playing". Factories with unspent funds and an empty tile also count as "not done" in that message.
- Build menu: select an empty owned factory; a panel lists the five units with cost, a one-line role, and a disabled state
  with the reason ("Needs 150, you have 100").
- Commander: a start-of-battle card chooses the commander (three cards with passive and power in plain words), remembered
  for the day. A "Power" button appears from day 2 and shows what it will do; it is disabled once used.
- Objective banner (top, always on): "Capture the enemy HQ. Progress 8 of 20. Day 3 of 6." Funds shown as "Funds 300 (+300 a
  day)".
- A first-visit coach overlay (dismissible, remembered in localStorage, with try/catch) walks through: pick a commander,
  select a unit, move, attack with preview, end day. A permanent "How to play" disclosure holds the same text and the key
  list. The old legend becomes a compact terrain and unit reference inside that disclosure.
- Enemy phase: each enemy action highlights briefly (move, shot, capture, build) with a one-line log entry, then control
  returns; any click or key skips the animation. Reduced motion: no animation, the log still lists everything.
- Accessibility: every tile button keeps a descriptive `aria-label` ("Row 3, column 4, forest, your Tank, 8 HP, not yet acted");
  an `aria-live` log; the cursor, menus and tooltips are reachable and operable by keyboard; never colour alone.
- Mobile at 360 px: the iso stage scales to the container width; tap a unit to select, tap a tile to move, a bottom
  sheet shows the action menu and the preview (no hover dependence); tap targets of at least 24 px are guaranteed by
  hit areas that overlap into a unit's tile; pinch zoom is not needed.

## Art

Isometric sprites in `site/assets/games/frontlink/` through the existing pack.json loader (see `docs/games-sprites.md`);
vector fallbacks must exist for everything so the game works without any image. v2 needs a different sprite frame: the
generated terrain tiles are about 240 by 233 px with a visible dirt edge (tile top is a 2:1 diamond), units are about
170 px wide. Define the sprite manifest around that (do not squash the art), list new names for what is not yet drawn
(city and factory in neutral, player and enemy colours; bazooka and scout units in both colours; bridge) and make the code
fall back to vector drawings for those until the art exists.
