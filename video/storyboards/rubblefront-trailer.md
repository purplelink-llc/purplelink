# RubbleFront trailer: storyboard

Written first, before the JSON (`rubblefront-trailer.json`). Source plan: `docs/launch/marketing/steam-page-package.md` section 3 in the
Frontlink repo (the 13-shot, 84 second plan), shortened to what could be filmed from the game as it is on `main`.

Format: 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 and AAC. About 86 seconds. Gameplay is on screen from the first frame. Voiceover is synthetic
(edge-tts) and the Steam and YouTube descriptions say so. Music is the game's own title theme, generated by the game's audio code;
sound effects are the game's own, recorded with the footage. Nothing third-party.

Rules held in every shot: no medic on screen, no real flags or insignia, no achievement toast, no tutorial overlay, no desktop, no file
names, the date is only "Spring 2027", every hit-chance number on screen is one the game computed (10 to 95 percent), no claim beyond
the verified feature list, no "hand-drawn" or "painted", no Steam Deck or controller claim.

## How the footage is made

Godot's movie writer records the real game at 1920x1080, 30 fps, one scenario per launch (`game/tools/trailer_runner.gd` in the Frontlink
repo). The scenario drives the same input paths a player uses: select a unit, choose a tile, Attack, pick a target, read the preview,
Fire. Units are placed on the board before the first move and a wall may start with fewer hit points; every die rolled after that is the
game's own, from the seed. A headless probe (`trailer_probe.gd`) plays the same moves first so a take with the wanted outcome is chosen
by seed, not edited. Scratch save id `trailer`, so no real save is touched. The clip list below says which shots are live footage.

## Voiceover and captions

Calm, plain, specific, spoken lines. No buzzwords, no "not X but Y". Two voices were tried (en-US-AndrewNeural and en-GB-RyanNeural);
both samples are in the output folder for the owner to listen to. Narration is normalised to -16 LUFS per clip. Burned-in captions
follow the word timing from the synthesis, so they match what is said.

## Music and sound plan

- Bed: `game/audio/music/title.ogg` (100 bpm march, 125 s, made by the game's generator). Starts at 0:00, ducked 7 dB under the voice.
- Effects: the game's own effect audio recorded in each clip at the moment it happens (gunshot, hit, explosion, overwatch cue, build
  cues). In-game effects are mixed 4 dB down so the voice stays clear. Accessibility cue tones are off.
- No third-party music or samples. Nobody has listened to the generated music in a review pass (known-issues.md); the owner should listen
  to the whole mix before it is uploaded.

## Cutdowns

- 30 s, 16:9: shots 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and the end card, shortened, with narration.
- 15 s, 9:16: hook, hit, wall to rubble, overwatch, end card. Music and effects, captions as text cards, no voiceover.
- Hook variants (8 s each, 16:9, no voiceover): A the odds, B a 90 percent shot that misses, C the wall to rubble. Each is the opening
  of the main trailer with a different first five seconds.
- Web hero loop: silent, 1280x720, under 1.5 MB as webm and mp4, a seamless loop of gameplay.
