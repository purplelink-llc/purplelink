Vitae

Academic research management for macOS. Track submissions, grants, projects, collaborators, and your CV in one local-first app. Free, notarized, no App Store.

Download for Mac Vitae Plus, 7 days free

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later · Apple-notarized · Your data stays on your Mac

What's inside

Everything an academic needs, nothing they don't

Submission tracking

Log every journal and conference submission with status, dates, each review round, and the decision. Pick target venues with published acceptance rates for about 13,700 journals and conferences, and get a forecast of publication timelines from your own submission history.

Grant management

Track NSF, NIH, and other grants from planning to award: deadlines, requested and awarded amounts, reporting dates, and your share. Search Grants.gov for open funding opportunities from inside the app.

CV & packages

Start from the CV you have. Vitae imports it from Word, PDF, RTF, ODT, or a web page, in APA, Chicago, MLA, IEEE, ACM, Harvard, or Vancouver style, reads your papers, grants, positions, talks, teaching and service, and shows you everything before saving. From then on your CV fills itself from your records. Choose from seven CV formats or build your own, and export several at once to PDF, LaTeX, or rich text that opens in Word. Word (.docx) templates are part of Vitae Plus.

Inbox triage

Connect iCloud, Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Fastmail, or your university mail, read-only. Vitae recognizes messages from journal submission systems, reads them on your Mac, and proposes submission updates. You review each one before anything changes.

Collaborators & projects

Track coauthors, collaborators, and projects. Link people to papers, grants, and roles so your network stays navigable. Send any publication to Zotero in one click.

Local-first & private

Everything is stored on your Mac with SwiftData. No account and no cloud sync required, and keeping your record needs no subscription. Vitae takes a daily backup and lets you undo changes, so a bad import or merge is never permanent.

Free & open to support

Vitae is free. If it saves you time, consider supporting it.

All core record-keeping is free, with no limits, and nothing that was free before Plus becomes paid. No third-party ads, no data harvesting. Vitae Plus is an optional subscription, $3 a month or $24 a year, for newer tools such as reports, Word templates, the revision workspace and Shareables. Frequently asked questions

Is Vitae really free?
Yes. Vitae is free to download and use, with all core record-keeping and no limits, and nothing that was free before Plus becomes paid. An optional subscription, Vitae Plus ($3 a month or $24 a year, with a 7-day free trial), adds newer tools: reports such as an NIH biosketch draft, Word export with your own templates, the revision workspace, Shareables, the collaboration map, batch DOI lookup and ORCID sync, Reminders and calendar sync, and themes. You do not need it to keep your record. A tip via Buy Me a Coffee is also appreciated but never required.
Does Vitae show ads?
Not from anyone else. The sidebar has one small card, labelled "From Purplelink", that suggests another Purplelink tool such as ModernTex, Paper Review or Vitae Plus. If a sponsor ever appears there, the card will say "Sponsor" and the sponsor's name, and this page will say so. There is no ad network, no tracking, and no click reporting, and Vitae sends nothing to sponsors. Vitae reads the card's text from a plain file on purplelink.llc at most once a day, and shows a copy built into the app when you are offline. You can hide the card in Settings, under Support; Vitae then stops fetching it.
How does Vitae update?
From version 0.3, Vitae checks purplelink.llc once a day and asks before installing a new version. Updates are signed, and Vitae refuses any that fail the check. You can turn the daily check off, or check by hand, in Settings under About. Version 0.1 cannot update itself; download the current version once from this page and it will keep itself up to date after that.
Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?
Distributing outside the App Store lets me ship faster and avoid App Store restrictions. The DMG is Apple-notarized, so macOS Gatekeeper verifies it is safe to run. Just open the DMG, drag Vitae to Applications, and launch it.
What macOS version is required?
Vitae requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
Where is my data stored?
All your records are stored locally on your Mac using SwiftData, with no account and no sync. Vitae goes online for lookups you ask for, such as citation counts from Crossref or OpenAlex and Grants.gov funding searches, and reads your email only if you connect a mailbox, read-only. It also makes two small requests to purplelink.llc on its own: the daily update check, and the daily fetch of the sidebar card's text unless you hide the card. Neither sends anything about you or your records. iCloud sync is supported in the schema and can be enabled in future versions.
How do I install it?
Download the DMG, open it, drag Vitae into your Applications folder, and launch it. Because it is notarized by Apple, you will not see a Gatekeeper warning.
How does Vitae compare to Interfolio, ORCID, or a spreadsheet?
Vitae keeps your own record between deadlines; if your university requires Interfolio or Watermark for tenure review, you still submit there, using what Vitae exports. See how to track your academic CV and tenure case for a comparison of sixteen options.
