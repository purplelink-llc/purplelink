About Purplelink

Atlanta, Georgia · Founded 2026 · Georgia LLC

Purplelink LLC is a one-person software studio building Mac apps and manuscript tools for academic researchers, plus iOS apps for travel and health. The mission is simple: make software that solves real problems and lasts, not software that ships fast and deteriorates.

Who makes this

I'm Benjamin Ampel, a researcher in cybersecurity, AI and information systems, based in Atlanta. I've spent a lot of time in LaTeX: writing manuscripts, watching compile errors scroll by, checking references by hand before a deadline. The apps that stuck, for me, were always the ones that solved a specific problem I had, for people I understood. Purplelink is where I build those.

It is a one-person studio, so the person who answers your email is the person who wrote the code. If something is broken or missing, write to ben@purplelink.llc.

What we build

ModernTex, Vitae, GlobePin and the web tools are shipping. Haea is in active development:

ModernTex

A native macOS LaTeX studio for academic researchers. Multi-file editing, synchronized PDF preview, BibTeX autocomplete, and submission-readiness checks built for the manuscript workflow. Shipping since September 2026, now at version 1.3; a free 7-day trial, then $10 once.

Vitae

A local-first Mac app for academics: submissions, grants, projects and your CV in one place. Free, with an optional Vitae Plus subscription from $3 a month.

Paper Review

A pre-submission read of your manuscript: four AI reviewers, a check of every reference against CrossRef, and a figure scan. From $9, no account, nothing kept after delivery.

Free tools

LaTeX to PDF, BibTeX validation, citation generation, LaTeX diff and more, in your browser. Plus Scholar Utility Belt, a free Chrome extension for Google Scholar.

Haea

An on-device iOS health analytics platform. Advanced ML models for recovery, circadian rhythm, and biological age, all running locally with no cloud sync.

GlobePin

An iOS travel tracking app for mapping everywhere you've been, every flight taken, and every destination on the list. 3D globe, stats, goals, and shareable postcards. Version 1.0 shipped in September 2026, free on the App Store.

How we build

Native first

Every app is written in Swift for the Apple platform it belongs on. No cross-platform shortcuts. Fast, native, and coherent.

Privacy by design

Haea runs fully on-device. No cloud sync, no analytics SDKs, no data sold. Privacy isn't a policy. It's an architecture decision.

Craft over speed

Software that lasts takes more time to build. The tradeoff is worth it: apps people actually keep on their phones and open every day.

Small and focused

Purplelink is a one-person studio. Small means accountable: every line of code and every design decision has an owner.
