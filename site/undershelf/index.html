Undershelf: a Mac mini mount for under your desk

A printed cradle that holds a Mac mini M4 or M5 under a desk, with the floor left open so the Mac breathes room air, the power button in reach and a drilling template in the box. It is not for sale yet. Join the list and I will write once, when it is.

What it does

  • Leaves the Mac's base open. The M4 and M5 Mac mini draw air in through the bottom. The cradle holds the Mac by its edges, so nothing sits under the vent.
  • Keeps the power button in reach. The button is on the underside near the back. With the floor open you reach it from below.
  • Holds the Mac with a front ridge. It slides in, drops behind a small ridge and stays put. Lift the front a millimetre or two to take it out.
  • Prints in one piece with no supports. It is printed standing on its front face, so the Mac's weight runs along the printed layers, not across them.
  • Comes with a 1:1 drilling template and a 50 mm check bar, so the four screws land where they should.

How it is being tested

The shape came out of structural simulation (how far the walls open under load, what each screw carries) and airflow simulation (how much of its own warm exhaust the Mac breathes back in). The guide Mac mini under a desk: does it run hotter? explains what the airflow work found.

Simulation is not a test. Before anything is sold, a printed cradle gets a physical hang test, and a Mac mini runs a 20-minute load in it and on a desk, logging chip temperature and fan speed. I will publish both results on this page. Until then there is no load rating here, on purpose.

Questions

Which Mac mini does it fit?

The 2024 and later Mac mini (M4, M4 Pro and the M5 models in the same case), 127 by 127 by 50 mm. It does not fit the older, larger Mac mini.

What do I need to install it?

Four #8 (M4) wood screws about 16 mm long with washers, a drill with a 3 mm bit and a screwdriver. The screws must be shorter than your desk is thick. Fit it to solid wood or a solid board, not to a hollow or honeycomb desk top.

Will the Mac run hotter under a desk?

In the airflow simulation, a Mac in this cradle breathed air about 1 C warmer than the room under sustained load, because the floor is open. The physical test will check that on a real Mac. The guide has the details and the general rules for any under-desk mount.

Can I print it myself?

Not yet. A file may follow once the printed version has been tested and sold.
