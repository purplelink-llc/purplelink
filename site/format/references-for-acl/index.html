ACL reference format

ACL venues and ACL Rolling Review use the official ACL style files: acl.sty with acl_natbib.bst. References must carry a DOI where one exists, or an ACL Anthology link as second choice.

Checked 2026-10-01 against the sources linked at the end of this page.

What ACL expects

  • Reference style: Author-year through natbib. acl.sty loads natbib and sets \bibliographystyle{acl_natbib} itself.
  • Style file: \usepackage[review]{acl} for a review version, \usepackage{acl} for the final version, on the 11pt article class.
  • DOIs: All references are required to contain DOIs when possible, or links to ACL Anthology pages as a second resort.
  • Length: Long papers: 8 pages of content in review, 9 in the final version. Short papers: 4 and 5. References are unlimited in both.
  • Required section: A section titled “Limitations” before the references. It does not count toward the page limit, and ARR desk-rejects papers without it.
  • Review: Two-way anonymized. No author names or affiliations, and no self-references that reveal identity.

Setting up the bibliography

With the ACL style file you do not write a \bibliographystyle line. At the end of the document:

\documentclass[11pt]{article}
\usepackage[review]{acl}
...
\bibliography{custom}

Keep acl_natbib.bst next to your .tex file. The kit also includes anthology.bib, the bibliography for the ACL Anthology.

LaTeX template

The official kit is the acl-org/acl-style-files repository, also available as an Overleaf template linked from its README. Its README says authors may not modify the style files or use templates designed for other conferences.

Common mistakes

  • Entries without a doi or url field. The formatting guidelines require a DOI where possible, or an Anthology link.
  • Lost capitals. acl_natbib.bst lowercases titles, so “NLU”, “BART” and “CheckList” above only survive because they are in braces in the .bib entry.
  • Leaving out the Limitations section. ARR desk-rejects papers without one.
  • Self-references such as “We previously showed (Smith, 1991)”. ARR asks for “Smith previously showed (Smith, 1991)”.

Frequently asked

What bibliography style does ACL use?

acl_natbib.bst, from the official ACL style files. The acl.sty file sets it, so the document needs only a \bibliography line.

Do references count toward the ACL page limit?

No. Long papers have 8 pages of content and short papers 4 in review, plus unlimited pages for references.

Does ACL require DOIs in references?

Yes. The formatting guidelines require DOIs for all cited works when possible, or links to ACL Anthology pages as a second resort.
