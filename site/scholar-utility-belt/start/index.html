Installed

Scholar Utility Belt is ready

Open Google Scholar and search for anything. Journal rankings, citation trends and author metrics appear under each result, with nothing else to switch on.

Open Google Scholar

Three things to try

Run a search

Each result gets colored badges for the rankings that list its venue: ABDC, FT50, UTD24, SJR quartiles and others. A venue only gets a badge for a ranking that includes it.

Open an author profile

Author pages show the h-index and g-index among other metrics, and how many of the author's papers sit in top-ranked venues.

Open the settings

Right-click the extension's icon in the toolbar and choose Options. If you cannot see the icon, open the puzzle-piece menu and pin Scholar Utility Belt. There you choose which rankings show, the theme and the badge colors. The same FAQ is at the top of that page.

Questions people ask

I do not see any badges.
Check that you are on a Google Scholar results or author page, that Show venue quality badges is on under Quality Badges in the extension's Options, and then reload the page. A journal can have one badge and not another, because a ranking only badges the venues it lists.
Does it send my data anywhere?
There are no accounts and no tracking. A few optional features look things up in public scholarly services such as OpenAlex, Crossref and Unpaywall, only when you turn them on. Everything the extension stores stays in your browser. The full list is on the Scholar Utility Belt page and in the privacy policy.
What is free, and what is Pro?
The core is free. Pro is optional and adds author compare, citation lineage and a few research workspaces, with a 14-day free trial. The Pro card in the extension's Options shows your plan.
Something is not working.
Open an issue on GitHub or email ben@purplelink.llc. Say which Scholar page you were on and what you expected to see.

About this page

This page opens once, right after you install the extension. The extension itself sends nothing to Purplelink. A visit to this page is counted the way every page on this site is, without cookies or an account, so we can tell roughly how many people install it.
