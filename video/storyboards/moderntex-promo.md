# ModernTex promo: storyboard

Target: about 27 seconds as rendered (narration plus lead and tail). 16:9 for the site and YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. A 6 second silent hero loop for the ModernTex page is cut from scene 2. Facts come only from `site/llms.txt` and `site/moderntex/index.html`. No statistics, ratings, testimonials or user counts.

Creative assumptions: the narrator is a calm male US voice (edge-tts, en-US-AndrewNeural). Screens are the existing marketing screenshots, so the "clip" scenes are stills with camera moves until real recordings replace them. Scene length is the narration plus a short lead and tail, so the timings below are approximate.

## 1. Hook (about 4 s)
- **Sees:** deep purple frame. The headline sets in word by word, then holds.
- **Understands:** this is about LaTeX errors, and there is a plain-language answer.
- **On-screen copy:** "LaTeX errors, in plain language."
- **Narration:** "LaTeX errors are hard to read. ModernTex explains them plainly."
- **Connects to next:** a short crossfade from purple to the light product scene.

## 2. Product reveal (about 6 s)
- **Sees:** the full ModernTex window on a light background, source on the left and PDF on the right. Slow zoom in. A ring appears around the PDF preview when the narration says "PDF".
- **Understands:** a native Mac editor, compiles locally, preview stays in sync.
- **On-screen copy:** "A native Mac editor."
- **Narration:** "A native Mac editor. It compiles locally and keeps the PDF preview in sync."
- **Connects to next:** same window family; the next scene pushes in to the bottom of a window.

## 3. Close-up: the error (about 5 s)
- **Sees:** a tight crop of the build results panel, filling the frame, ring on the error row.
- **Understands:** a failed build shows the error with a plain explanation, right in the app.
- **On-screen copy:** "Errors you can read."
- **Narration:** "When a build fails, the error comes with a plain explanation."
- **Connects to next:** crossfade to a text-led layout.

## 4. Three checks (about 7 s)
- **Sees:** three rows appear one at a time, each timed to its words. Title above, short detail under each row.
- **Understands:** submission checks, revision snapshots, BibTeX completion.
- **On-screen copy:** "Submission-readiness checks / Anonymization, page limits, required sections", "Revision snapshots / Take one before a revision or after review", "BibTeX completion / Searches by author, title or key".
- **Narration:** "Submission checks, revision snapshots, and BibTeX completion."
- **Connects to next:** the last row holds, then a crossfade to the end card.

## 5. End card (about 7 s, settles)
- **Sees:** dark frame, a small screenshot at right, Purplelink logo, "ModernTex", price line, URL.
- **Understands:** free for 7 days, then $10 once; macOS 14 or later.
- **On-screen copy:** "Free for 7 days, then $10 once." "macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel. Notarized." "purplelink.llc/moderntex"
- **Narration:** "Free for seven days, then ten dollars, once. macOS 14 or later."
- **Ends:** holds on the URL for about two seconds after the voice stops.
