# Photo contest shortlist (cash prizes, entries open 2026-09-24 to 2027-03)

Researched 2026-09-24 from each contest's own rules page (primary source). Nothing was entered or paid.

Profile this was checked against: travel, landscape, architecture, nature and night-sky images that are
already licensed **non-exclusively** on Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, iStock, Alamy, Dreamstime and 123RF,
sold as prints on Fine Art America, and in some cases processed with AI denoise, sharpening or upscaling
(Topaz, Lightroom Enhance).

**Status labels**
- **Verified**: the current edition's rules were read on the organizer's site.
- **Expected**: the next edition has not been posted yet. Dates and fees are extrapolated from the
  2025/26 edition, whose rules were read. Re-read the new rules before paying.

**How to read "Stock-licensed OK?"** No rules page that was read bans an entrant's own non-exclusive
stock licensing, except APOTY, which discourages it (see below). "Yes" means the rules put no
restriction on prior publication or licensing, or they explicitly allow published work. It does not
mean the rules say "stock is fine" in those words. Two rules that look like stock bans are not:
Pano Awards and The Nature Photography Contest prohibit *purchased third-party stock elements* inside
your image. Your own licensed photo is not affected.

**Value** = top cash prize ÷ cheapest fee that makes you eligible for it. The currencies are the same
on both sides, so no FX conversion is needed. Free contests rank first.

## Recommended entries

1. **International Landscape Photographer of the Year (by Oct 24, 2026).** This is the best fit for the
   portfolio. It is the only contest that explicitly allows AI noise reduction, it has no stock or
   publication restriction, the rights are clean, and it has a dedicated $500 Night Award. Enter 5
   images for $100 (5th free before Oct 24). That qualifies you for the $5,000 Photographer award as
   well as the $2,000 Photograph award. Files are 4000–5000 px, so no upscaling is needed.
2. **Sony World Photography Awards, Single Image (by Jan 5, 2027).** It's free: pick your 3 best
   **2026-captured** images across Landscape, Architecture and Travel. Describe any Topaz or Enhance
   processing in the image description, as the rules require.
3. **HIPA "Water: Our Shared Story" (by Oct 15, 2026).** It's free and has the biggest cash prize
   here ($10k). Enter only if you can build a coherent 5–10 image water story. Use lightly edited,
   non-upscaled masters. Before entering, accept that winning grants HIPA a perpetual, sub-licensable
   licence for any purpose.
4. **BigPicture 2027 (Dec 2026 to ~Mar 2027).** $25 covers 10 images, with $1k per category and $5k
   grand. Enter single-frame landscapes only: stitched panos and HDR are banned.
5. **Travel Photographer of the Year (by Oct 12, 2026).** Enter a Landscapes portfolio (£28) plus one
   or two In Nature One Shots (£14). The rights are clean and denoise is allowed. Don't enter
   stitched panoramas without written approval. Use **Monochrome (Nov 15)** instead or as well if you
   have strong B&W work.

**Skip Smithsonian.** Its licence lets Smithsonian sell products with your image for free, forever,
which works directly against the Fine Art America print sales. **APOTY** only makes sense for
night-sky frames you have *not* put on stock.

## Epson International Pano Awards: priority, but closed until mid-2027

The 2026 edition closed **2026-07-13** (early bird Jun 22). It opened around mid-May 2026, so the 2027
edition is unlikely to open before March 2027. Plan for **May–June 2027**. What the 2026 rules say,
and what to prepare now:

- **Fee:** $22 per image in Open. 20% off 5+ images, early-bird only.
- **Prize pool:** over $50k, including $15k cash. Open Photographer of the Year gets $3,000 cash plus
  about $7.5k in products. Category/special awards (Raw Planet, Curator's) are $1,000 cash.
- **Categories:** Nature/Landscape, Built Environment, Aerial. Minimum ratio is 2:1.
- **Stock:** your own licensed images are OK. The only related rule bans *purchased stock elements*
  inside an entry. There is no date-of-origin limit. Images already top-10 or major-award in past Pano
  Awards are excluded.
- **Rights:** non-exclusive and irrevocable. Exhibition and web use in perpetuity; press
  sub-licensing for 5 yrs. No commercial grab.
- **AI:** "generative and instructional AI content of any kind" is banned, and they may request RAW
  files. "Instructional AI" is undefined, so Topaz upscaling is the main risk. Entry files are exactly
  3000 px on the long side, so export from non-upscaled stitched masters and keep every source RAW.

## Pre-entry checklist (applies to every contest above)

- Keep the original RAW for every entry. Almost every contest here demands it at shortlist stage and
  compares it against the entry.
- Enter from **non-upscaled** masters. Denoise is allowed at ILPOTY, TPOTY, WPY, APOTY (declared) and
  BigPicture. Upscaling is the risky step everywhere.
- Declare processing where asked (Sony, APOTY).
- Farmani contests (IPA, BIFA, TIFA) treat anyone with published work as **Professional**. Stock
  licensing counts, so budget for the Pro fee.
- Before paying, re-open the rules URL. Several rows are "Expected" and the organizer can change fees
  and terms up to launch.
