Dyrhólaey

A headland near Vík with a natural arch big enough to sail through, cliffs full of puffins in summer and a view east along the black beaches toward Reynisdrangar.

Dyrhólaey is a 120-metre headland near Vík; its name means door-hill island, after the arch.
