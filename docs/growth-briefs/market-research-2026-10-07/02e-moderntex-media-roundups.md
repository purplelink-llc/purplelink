## Notes for Ben

- Best realistic first steps: Indie App Catalog non-store form (free, Mac-friendly), a short personal email to MacStories (viticci@ / voorhees@), and a short note to TidBITS (ace@). All three are published routes.
- Hard no-pitch policies: Thesis Whisperer, TidBITS (guest/sponsored posts), Macworld (guest posts/unsolicited submissions), Six Colors (no route). Respect them.
- Only outlets that explicitly invite product review submissions on their published pages: Macworld ("product review submissions" to the listed editors, but "we cannot respond to all queries") and Cult of Mac (reviews@, with the mailto-subject caveat).
- Your own guide https://purplelink.llc/guides/best-mac-latex-editors/ already ranks in search results for "best LaTeX editors for Mac" next to MacMyths, Octree and slant.co, so roundup authors may find it without a pitch.
