License a photograph

For websites, publications, marketing and other commercial use, the photographs are licensed through stock agencies. Two of them have public portfolios you can browse.

  • iStock portfolioCreative and editorial photographs, licensed through iStock, part of Getty Images.→
  • Dreamstime portfolioA selection of travel and landscape photographs, licensed through Dreamstime.→

The photographs are also licensed through Adobe Stock, Shutterstock and Alamy. If you cannot find the one you want, or you need a larger file or terms an agency does not offer, write to ben@purplelink.llc and I will reply directly.
