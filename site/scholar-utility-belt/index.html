Chrome extension, free core

Scholar Utility Belt

Scholar Utility Belt is a Chrome extension that adds journal quality badges, citation velocity, author metrics, lineage trees and retraction alerts to Google Scholar results. The core is free and open source. Since version 0.6.0 a few workflow tools are part of an optional Pro tier. The ranking lists ship inside the extension, so badges need no network request.

What it adds

Context next to every result

Journal quality badges

ABDC, FT50, UTD24 and SJR rankings shown beside each result, so you can see where a paper appeared without looking the venue up.

Citation velocity

How quickly each paper is being cited, shown next to its total citation count.

Author metrics

h-index and g-index for authors, shown on their Google Scholar profiles.

Academic lineage

Academic lineage trees that place an author among their advisors and students.

Retraction alerts

A visible warning on results that have been retracted, before you cite them.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no remote lookups for rankings. The source is open under the ISC license, so you can read exactly what it does.

Free and Pro

What is free and what is Pro

Reading and browsing aids stay free: quality badges, the result action grid, citation velocity, the saved-paper library, author summary metrics (h-index, g-index and similar), the query trend tracker, retraction and tortured-phrase warnings, and related works. Pro covers workflow tools that save power users time.

Pro features

Author compare, citation lineage, extended bibliometrics (p-index, FWCI, Relative Citation Ratio, influential citations), narrative CV, the systematic-review workspace, the citation-graph overlay and the Publish-or-Perish report.

Prices

$40 once (lifetime), or $3 a month, or $20 a year. Payment goes through ExtensionPay and Stripe.

If you installed before 0.6.0

Every feature you had stays free, permanently. Updating from an earlier version marks your install as free for good. A fresh install, including reinstalling after removing the extension, counts as new.

Privacy and network calls

What the extension sends, and to whom

The extension has no Purplelink server and no analytics. It sends no searches, reading history or saved papers to Purplelink. Your library and settings stay in the extension's storage in your browser. It does make these requests from your browser:

  • Public scholarly services, for optional enrichment. Depending on the features you enable, it queries OpenAlex, Crossref, Unpaywall, OpenCitations, Semantic Scholar, DBLP, NIH iCite, PubMed, Europe PMC and ROR. These requests carry what the lookup needs, such as a DOI, a title, an author name or a search term, and go straight from your browser to that service.
  • ExtensionPay, for the Pro license check. ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) sells and verifies Pro licenses, with Stripe processing payment. If you never open its payment or sign-in page, the extension makes no request to extensionpay.com. When you click Unlock or "Already purchased", the extension asks ExtensionPay for a random per-install key and stores it in the extension's storage, which Chrome may sync through your Google account. After that, each time the extension checks your Pro status it sends that key to extensionpay.com and receives your plan and paid status, which it keeps locally. On extensionpay.com pages, an ExtensionPay script passes payment confirmation back to the extension. ExtensionPay and Stripe see the key and your IP address like any web request, plus whatever you enter at checkout. Purplelink never sees card details.
  • No ExtensionPay calls for early installs. Installs that were updated from a version before 0.6.0 skip the license check entirely.

The full policy is in the Purplelink privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scholar Utility Belt free?
The core is free and open source under the ISC license. Since version 0.6.0 there is also an optional Pro tier for people who install for the first time: $40 once, or $3 a month, or $20 a year. Anyone who installed before 0.6.0 keeps every earlier feature free.
Which features are Pro?
Author compare, citation lineage, the extended bibliometrics (p-index, FWCI, Relative Citation Ratio and influential citations), narrative CV, the systematic-review workspace, the citation-graph overlay and the Publish-or-Perish report.
I installed it before Pro existed. Do I have to pay?
No. Installs that were updated to version 0.6.0 or later from an earlier version keep every feature free permanently. A fresh install, including reinstalling after removing the extension, is treated as a new install.
Does it track what I search?
There is no account and no analytics, and nothing about your searches or reading is sent to Purplelink. Ranking badges are looked up locally. Optional lookups go to public scholarly services, and the Pro license check goes to ExtensionPay; both are described under Privacy and network calls above.
Which journal lists does it show?
ABDC, the Financial Times 50 (FT50), the UT Dallas 24 (UTD24) and SCImago Journal Rank (SJR).
Which browsers does it support?
Google Chrome, from the Chrome Web Store.
