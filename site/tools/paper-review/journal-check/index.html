Journal Check

A Standard Paper Review runs without a target journal. This check takes the same manuscript PDF and compares it with one journal's stated rules: abstract length, body word count, page count, required and recommended sections, and reference style. It is the checklist the Journal tier includes, sold on its own.

  1. Pay through Stripe
  2. Choose the journal and upload the manuscript PDF
  3. Read the checklist on the page, usually in under a minute

Secure checkout via Stripe. One-time payment.

The check is rule-based. No AI reviewer reads the manuscript, and it is not sent to any model provider. The result is deleted from our servers within about a day after you first open it, or within 48 hours if you never do. If it is not useful, we will refund the $10.

Do not upload a manuscript, proposal or application you are reviewing for someone else, or anything shared with you in confidence.

What it checks

  • Abstract word count against the journal's limit.
  • Main text word count and page count, where the journal sets one.
  • Sections the journal requires or recommends, such as methods, limitations or a data availability statement.
  • The reference style the journal asks for, as a reminder to confirm by hand.

Counts taken from a PDF are approximate. Confirm any number near a limit in your own editor, and read the journal's current author guidelines before you submit.
