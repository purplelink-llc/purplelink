Bern

The old town fills a loop of the Aare: arcaded streets, the Zytglogge clock tower, the cathedral spire and the red roofs seen from the Rose Garden.

Bern's old town has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1983; its arcades run for about six kilometres.
