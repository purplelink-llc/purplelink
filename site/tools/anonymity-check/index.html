# Anonymity Check
**Result: 6 potential leaks detected.**
Each item below should be removed or generalised before double-blind submission.

## Self-citation patterns
- **[CRITICAL]** Section 2, related work: "In our previous work [14], we found that review accuracy declines after the first hour of a session."
  - Fix: Rewrite in the third person ("Prior work [14] found ...") and keep [14] as an ordinary reference.

## Institutions
- **[MAJOR]** methods section: "Participants were recruited from the professional master's program at [University Name]."
  - Fix: Replace the name with "a large public university".

## IRB and ethics protocol numbers
- **[MAJOR]** methods section, ethics statement: "The study was approved by the [University Name] IRB under protocol 2024-0917."
  - Fix: Write "approved by the authors' institutional review board" and withhold the protocol number until acceptance.

## Funding and grants
- **[MAJOR]** page 14 acknowledgements: "This work was supported by NSF grant #[Grant Number]."
  - Fix: Remove the acknowledgements from the review copy and restore them in the camera-ready version.

## Emails and URLs
- **[CRITICAL]** page 6 footnote 3: "Materials and analysis scripts are available at github.com/[username]/sleep-review-study."
  - Fix: Link an anonymized copy of the repository for review, with commit history and author names removed.

## Named software, datasets and systems
- **[MINOR]** Section 3.2: "Defects were seeded with ReviewSeed, the tool we released last year."
  - Fix: Call it "a defect-seeding tool" and cite it as an anonymized reference.

What it catches

  • Author or co-author names in the body (e.g. "Smith et al." referring to the authors' own prior work).
  • Institution names: "at our institution X", lab names, hospital names.
  • Funder names and grant numbers ("NSF #1234567", "NIH R01…").
  • IRB / IACUC / ethics-board protocol numbers tied to a specific institution.
  • Author email addresses, author-owned GitHub repos, lab webpages.
  • Named software or datasets that trivially identify the authors.
  • "In our previous work [12]" self-citation patterns.

FAQ

What about acknowledgements?
We scan the body and abstract. Acknowledgement sections often live at the end of the manuscript and we'll catch leaks there too, but always review them manually before submission, especially if your editor strips them automatically.
Is this a replacement for manual blinding?
No. It's a safety net. Manual review of figures, supplementary materials, and metadata is still required.
What does Purplelink retain?
Nothing after delivery. The scan result is deleted from our servers the moment you retrieve it. The manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which retains inputs for 30 days for abuse monitoring, never for training.
