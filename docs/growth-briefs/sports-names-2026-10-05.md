# Sports game names: what turned up (2026-10-05)

A web search, not a trademark clearance. Nothing here is legal advice. A knockout search at the USPTO (and a short call with a trademark lawyer if you plan paid promotion) is the real check.

## Teamlink

- **TeamLinkt** is a funded sports team-management app and website (teamlinkt.com, Saskatoon, since 2017), one letter away from "Teamlink" and in the same world (sports teams). Different product, but a plausible confusion argument.
- "Teammate Chain" is marked as a trademark by another maker (austinkrance.com), and Roster Relay, HOOPLINK, Dribble, Liney Link and Sports Link are all in the same space.
- Rosterlink is taken by a staff-shift app. Linemates is taken in spirit by Liney.
- No hits found for **Lockerlink**, **Benchlink** or **Huddlelink** as games (searched once each, only a pass).

Recommendation: rename, because of TeamLinkt. First choice **Lockerlink** (it says what the game is: people who shared a locker room). Keep the "-link" family sound.

## Gridlink

- No game or app called Gridlink turned up. Near neighbours: Gridlocked (a hockey, football and baseball trivia app series), GridSport, Gridiron Trivia, Crossover Grid.
- Recommendation: keep.

## Unbeaten

- **Go Unbeaten** (gounbeaten.com, apps) is a multi-sport perfect-season game with a daily challenge and leaderboard. Same word, same category.
- The whole genre is flooded: 82-0, 84-0, 98-0, 17-0, 20-0, 16-0, 40-0, 162-0, Perfect Season (several sites), Run The Games, All-Timers, X-0 Games, Cap Room (a daily budget game that shows the best lineup).
- What we have that most do not: a budget instead of a random spin, players from any era with open choice, real-teammate chemistry, honors-based ratings, a daily rule, and a weekly best-roster recap.
- "Perfect Season" is used by at least four sites, so it is not available in any practical sense.
- Recommendation: either keep **Unbeaten** and accept the overlap with Go Unbeaten, or rename to something that names the budget twist, such as **Under the Cap** (not searched yet). If you rename, do it before promoting.

## What a rename costs

URLs change (/games/teamlink/ and so on), so each old URL needs a redirect, and the share cards, the sitemap, `llms.txt`, schema, saved player data and the leaderboard keys all use the old name. It is a few hours of mechanical work now and a lot more after the games have inbound links.
