How the timer works

While the stream ran, it used 25 minutes of focus then a 5-minute break, with a focus block starting at :00 and :30 past every hour (UTC). The box above still runs that clock in your browser, on the same schedule, so you can use it with any music.

What the music is

Chiptune is the sound of classic video game consoles, played gently. Over about two hours the music drifts from soft, slow lofi up to driving boss-fight energy and back down, so a long session does not sound the same all the way through. An algorithm generates every track as it plays. The music is original, with no samples and nothing licensed.

Questions

What is Focus Radio?

A free radio of original chiptune music for studying, coding, writing and reading, played over a pixel-art study room with a Pomodoro timer. It is instrumental, with no vocals. The live stream is paused.

What kind of music is it?

Chiptune is the sound of classic video game consoles, played gently. Over about two hours the music drifts from soft, slow lofi (around 62 BPM, beats per minute) up to driving boss-fight energy (around 94 BPM) and back down, so a long session does not sound the same all the way through.

How does the Pomodoro timer work?

The stream ran 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5-minute break, with a focus block at :00 and :30 past every hour (UTC). Work during focus, then stand up, stretch and drink some water in the break.

Is the music copyrighted?

Every track is generated by an algorithm, so it is original, with no samples and nothing licensed from anyone else.

Is it free?

Yes. It is on YouTube and costs nothing. The live stream is paused; subscribing to the channel is the way to hear when it returns.

Who makes it?

Purplelink LLC, a one-person studio in Atlanta, Georgia, which also builds Mac apps and manuscript tools for researchers and students.
