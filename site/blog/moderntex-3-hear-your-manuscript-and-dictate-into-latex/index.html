ModernTex 3.0: hear your manuscript, and dictate into LaTeX

By Benjamin Ampel · November 13, 2026

Click anywhere in your LaTeX, press one button, and ModernTex reads from there with each sentence highlighted. Dictation goes the other way. Both run on your Mac.

ModernTex 3.0 adds two things I wanted for my own manuscripts: it reads your LaTeX aloud, and it takes dictation into your source. Both run on your Mac. Nothing is sent off it.

Read aloud, from wherever you click

Click anywhere in your LaTeX and press one button. ModernTex starts reading from that spot in the best voice installed on your Mac, and highlights each sentence as it goes. You see exactly where the voice is, so your eyes and ears stay together. Press Stop and it remembers your place, so the next press picks up where you left off.

It reads LaTeX as prose. Commands are skipped and maths is handled instead of spelled out symbol by symbol. Figure captions are read. A table is summarised from its own source, with no numbers added that the table does not contain. You can pause and resume, and skip a sentence back or forward when you want to hear a line again.

It works in Zen mode too. That is where I use it most, for proofreading by ear. A repeated word, a sentence that runs out of breath and a missing "the" are all easier to hear than to see once you have read a draft ten times. Reading it aloud makes the draft slower and therefore harder to skim past.

Dictation into LaTeX

Dictation turns speech into text at the caret, recognised on the Mac. It is built for LaTeX, so the characters that break a build are handled for you: %, &, _, $ and # are escaped. Capitals and spacing follow the caret (a sentence start is capitalised, and a word after a space does not get a second space). Spoken punctuation works, so you can say "comma" and "full stop" as you would in any dictation tool.

Each dictated passage goes in as one undo step. If you do not like it, one Undo removes the whole passage. Dictation does not cover maths. Dictate the prose and type the equations.

Privacy

Speech recognition and the voice both run on your Mac. Your manuscript is not uploaded for either feature, and no audio leaves the machine.

Try it on the section you least want to reread

ModernTex 3.0 is a free update for everyone who owns it. New to ModernTex? The trial is the complete app for seven days, with no account. After that it is $19.99 once, with all updates included. Pick the section you have read too many times and listen to it. You will probably catch something in the first page.

Download it on the ModernTex page, or read the full list of updates.
