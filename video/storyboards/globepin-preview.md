# GlobePin App Store preview and 16:9 cut (2026-10-31)

Two cuts from the same real screen recordings of GlobePin 1.5.1 (Simulator, iPhone 17 Pro Max,
seeded demo data: 60 places, home New York, never Ben's real history). Separate from the
September narrated promo (`globepin-promo.json`), which stays as it is.

## A. App Store preview (portrait, 886x1920, 30 fps, about 27 s, music bed, no narration)

Apple's rules for previews: footage captured from the app, no hands or device bezels, no
pricing claims, no references to other platforms. Captions are text over a calm background band.
No Purplelink bumper here (brand footage that is not the app).

## B. 16:9 cut (1920x1080, for YouTube and purplelink.llc/globepin)
