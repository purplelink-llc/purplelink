Upload, pay once through Stripe, and get the result in minutes on a page that updates itself. The file and the result are deleted from our servers soon after you open the result. If a result reads as low quality, email within 14 days and the price is refunded.

Paper Review

Four AI reviewers read the manuscript the way a panel would: methods, statistics, data integrity and the editor's view. Every reference is checked against CrossRef and the figures are scanned. Markdown report and annotated PDF.

Start a review → $6

Response Review

A reviewer panel reads your response letter against the original comments and flags missing or hand-waved replies.

Check my response → $3

Citation Gap

Finds prior work the manuscript should cite and doesn't, with a reason for each.

Find the gaps → $2

Anonymity Check

Flags names, institutions, funders, IRB numbers and self-citations that break double-blind review. Included free with Paper Review.

Cover Letter
