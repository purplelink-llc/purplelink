A small tactics round on a seven by six map. You have six days to destroy the enemy force or hold its headquarters, on the same battlefield as everyone else today.

Loading today's battlefield.

Keyboard: arrow keys move over the board, Enter selects your unit and then a tile, Escape goes back. Each unit acts once a day. Artillery cannot move and fire in the same turn.
