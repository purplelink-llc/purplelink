Revision Review

This review checks whether your revisions addressed the original Paper Review's findings. Upload the revised manuscript and the Markdown report you downloaded last time. The result lists which checklist items you fixed, which you missed, and whether the revision introduced any new problems.

  1. Pay through Stripe
  2. Upload the revised PDF and paste the Markdown from your earlier review; the link is also emailed
  3. Read the result on the page, or ask to be emailed when it is ready

Requires the Markdown report from your previous review. Save it before you close the page next time.

Deleted soon after delivery. The result is deleted from our servers within about a day after you first open it, or within 48 hours if you never do. If it reads as low-quality, we refund the $2.

What the report includes

  1. Address Tracker. Every item from the original Rectification Checklist is marked addressed, partially addressed, not addressed or not evaluable. Where a fix exists, the report quotes your revision.
  2. New Issues. Fixing one thing can break another (for example, a new experiment whose statistics were not run, or a trimmed paragraph that removed a key caveat). The Revision Review flags up to five new issues, with severity.
  3. Action Checklist. What still needs to change before resubmission.

FAQ

What if I lost the original Markdown report?
Run a fresh Paper Review on the revised manuscript (from $9) instead. We cannot recover prior reviews. They are deleted from our servers within about a day after you first open the result, or within 48 hours if you never do.
Why is this cheaper than a full review?
A revision review reuses most of the analysis you already paid for, so it costs less to run. The output is also shorter. It evaluates your revision against the original review and does not start a fresh red-team pass.
