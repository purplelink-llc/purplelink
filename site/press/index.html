About Purplelink LLC

Purplelink LLC is a one-person software studio in Atlanta, Georgia, founded in 2026 by Benjamin Ampel. It makes tools for academic researchers: ModernTex, a $10 native macOS LaTeX editor; Vitae, a free Mac app for tracking submissions, grants and your CV; Paper Review, an AI pre-submission manuscript review from $9; Scholar Utility Belt, a free Chrome extension for Google Scholar; and free in-browser LaTeX and BibTeX tools. It also makes GlobePin, a free iOS travel-mapping app, and Haea, an on-device iOS health app in development.

Products

ModernTex - macOS

A native macOS LaTeX manuscript studio for academic researchers. Multi-file editing, synchronized PDF preview, BibTeX autocomplete, plain-language error diagnostics, and submission-readiness checks. Built in Swift for macOS 14+. Shipping since September 2026, current version 1.3; free 7-day trial, then $10 one-time, every 1.x update included. purplelink.llc/moderntex

Vitae - macOS

A local-first academic research manager: journal and conference submissions, grants, projects, collaborators, and a CV generated from that record. Free, notarized, macOS 15 or later; optional Vitae Plus subscription from $3 a month. purplelink.llc/vitae

Paper Review - web

A pre-submission review of an academic manuscript: a four-persona AI reviewer panel, a check of every reference against CrossRef, and a figure-integrity scan, delivered as a Markdown report and an annotated PDF. From $9 per review, no account; the manuscript and review are deleted on delivery. purplelink.llc/tools/paper-review

Haea - iOS (in development)

An on-device iOS health analytics platform. Integrates sleep, nutrition, weight, and biometric data with advanced ML models including Kalman filtering and Granger causality analysis. All computation runs locally - no cloud sync, no third-party SDKs.

GlobePin - iOS

An iOS travel tracking app for logging places visited, flights taken, and destinations planned. Features a 3D globe view, statistics dashboard, travel goals, anniversary reminders, and shareable travel postcards via iCloud sync. Version 1.0 shipped 3 September 2026; free, iOS 17 or later.
