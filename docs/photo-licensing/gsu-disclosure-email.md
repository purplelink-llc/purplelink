**Subject:** Question re: amending an approved outside-activity disclosure (Purplelink LLC)

Hello,

I have an approved outside-activity disclosure on file for Purplelink LLC, a
single-member LLC I own that develops software and related tools.

The company is adding a second line of business: licensing my own photography
to stock-photo agencies and print-on-demand marketplaces. I want to confirm
whether this requires an amendment to my existing disclosure, or whether it
falls outside the prior-approval requirement.

A few details that may be relevant:

- The activity is photography I shoot on personal time with personally owned
  equipment. It does not relate to my research, teaching, or service
  responsibilities, and does not involve GSU students, data, facilities, or
  funding.
- No university time, equipment, or resources are used.
- Revenue is passive licensing income paid to the LLC already named in my
  approved disclosure.
- There is no relationship between this activity and any sponsored research or
  external partner I work with at GSU.

My reading of BOR Policy 8.2.15 is that prior approval attaches to compensated
outside activities that relate to an employee's expertise or responsibilities,
which this likely does not. But because it operates through the same entity
named in my existing disclosure, I would rather confirm than assume.

Could you let me know whether I should file an amendment, submit a new
disclosure, or whether no action is needed?
