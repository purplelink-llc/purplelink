Each calendar is a PDF you print yourself: a cover and twelve months, one photograph per page, in US Letter and A4 landscape. Zermatt and the Matterhorn, the Jungfrau region, Bern and the Alps.

Best of

What you get

  1. Two PDFs per calendar: US Letter landscape (11 by 8.5 inches, Sunday-first weeks) and A4 landscape (Monday-first weeks). Both are thirteen pages: a cover and twelve months.
  2. Photographs at 300 dots per inch for the printed size, with no watermark. Each month names the photograph and the place it was taken.
  3. A download page right after payment. The Stripe receipt links back to it, and Find a purchase emails the link again if you lose it. Keep the files; print as many copies as you like for yourself or as gifts.
  4. Personal printing only. The files may not be resold, redistributed or used in a product. For commercial use of any photograph, see licensing.
