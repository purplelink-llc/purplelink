## Notes for the caller

- Best two for a short, honest "please consider listing" note: UTK OIT (via the Submit a Request portal) and Reed CUS (cus@reed.edu). Both pages are Mac-aware and list only free editors.
- UTK states a scoped support policy naming TeXstudio and TeXShop; Reed has none.
- No page I fetched says "we do not list commercial software". None of the pages mention ModernTex.
- I did not reach Stanford, Princeton, Harvard, MIT, Chicago econ or other stats or physics department pages with both a LaTeX editor list and a published contact. Search budget ended before those could be found, and guessed URLs were 404.
- Several contacts above are help desks for that institution's own users. A cold email to them asks for a favour; keep it to one short message and say the app is paid, with a 7-day free trial.
