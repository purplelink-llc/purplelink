Paper Review plans

A plan gives your lab a fixed number of Paper Reviews each month. Each paper reviewed on a plan also gets one Revision Review and one Response Review. You can pay monthly or yearly, and a year costs the same as ten months.

Lab

$199 a month

or $1,990 a year

20 Paper Reviews each month. The lab lead gets the individual review links and shares them with members.

Lab Plus

$349 a month

or $3,490 a year

40 Paper Reviews each month, and a group link. Any member opens the group link and starts a review from the shared pool. The lab lead can replace the group link at any time.

Plans renew each month or each year until you cancel, and you can cancel at any time from the plan page. To pay by invoice or purchase order, email ben@purplelink.llc. The full plan terms are on the terms page.

What every plan includes

  • Each review includes the journal fit check and the anonymity check, and so does each review in a pack.
  • Reviews arrive in monthly batches, on yearly billing too. You can use each month's reviews until the end of the following month. After that they expire.
  • Every paper reviewed on the plan gets one Revision Review and one Response Review at no extra cost. The links arrive in the report email and on the plan page, and they stay valid for 12 months. The response letter draft is not included. It is sold separately on the Response Review page.
  • Each report is emailed to the address typed on the upload form, so every member gets their own report.
  • On the plan page you can see the reviews left and each month's links, turn renewal off or back on, and open the billing page for the card and invoices. You reach the plan page from a link sent by email.
  • You can cancel at any time. The plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for, and reviews already issued stay usable until their date. Yearly plans get a reminder email 30 days and 7 days before renewal.

Data handling

This section is the short version for a research office or ethics board. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Paper Review and the other paid checks
The manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, inputs are retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and are not used for model training. Reference titles and DOIs are sent to CrossRef. When the review finishes, the Paper Review report is emailed as a PDF through Resend to the address typed on the upload form. The manuscript is not emailed. Purplelink deletes the manuscript and the online copy within about a day after the result is first opened, or within 48 hours if it is never opened.
Clinical and patient data
Purplelink is not a HIPAA business associate. Do not upload protected health information or identifiable participant data. De-identify the manuscript before you upload it. Unpublished results are fine, because the manuscript is deleted within about a day after you open the result and Anthropic does not train on it.
The free tools
Files sit in a temporary folder on the Purplelink server only while the result is built. They are deleted after that.
ModernTex and Vitae
Both are desktop apps. Files and records stay on your Mac unless you use an optional feature that sends them. ModernTex's AI features use your own AI account. Vitae contacts only the services you connect (e.g., your mailbox or Zotero).
Payments
Stripe handles payments on its own checkout page. Purplelink never sees card details, and no account is created.
Journal AI policies
Publishers let authors use AI tools on their own manuscripts, with rules on disclosure. See what ten publisher and venue policies say.
