# New Channels: Terms Research

Researched 2026-09-24 for Purplelink LLC (Atlanta, GA). The portfolio is about 735 travel,
landscape and architecture photos, licensed non-exclusively on Adobe Stock, Shutterstock,
iStock/Getty, Alamy, Dreamstime and 123RF, with prints on Fine Art America (FAA).
Etsy is covered elsewhere. No signups, submissions or contact were made.

Confidence tags: **[P]** primary legal document or official help center, read directly.
**[H]** official marketing page or help-center text seen only through a search snippet.
**[3]** third-party source. **[U]** unverified or inferred. Confirm these before acting.

---

## Summary

| Channel | Verdict | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Pixsy | Go, with caveats | Free monitoring of 500 images is useful. Cases on stock-distributed images are high risk. |
| Copytrack | Skip | Primary terms were unreachable. It overlaps with Pixsy and has a poor reputation. |
| Zazzle | Go, with caveats | Free, and calendars fit the portfolio. Royalty is paid on the discounted price, less fees. |
| Picfair | Skip for now | There is no marketplace any more. It sells only through a paid Plus store you market yourself. |
| Vecteezy | Go, Pro licence only | 50% subscriber share and CSV/FTP upload. Never use the Free pool. |
| Magnific (Freepik) | Go-with-caveats (tail) | Premium-only, 50% share, low RPD; Freepik sells archive for AI training; Spanish withholding. |
| AI data buyers | Passive only | You already get paid through Adobe, Shutterstock and Alamy. Wirestock no longer suits this portfolio. The rest serve companies, not individuals. |

## 1. Pixsy (with a brief Copytrack comparison)

**Verdict: go, with caveats.** Use it as a detection tool on the free plan. File cases only
for images that were never on a microstock site, or where you have proof that the user
holds no licence.

**Key numbers**
- Free plan: 500 images monitored, "regular priority" scanning, 0 takedowns per month [P, pixsy.com/monitor].
  Pixsy publishes no scan frequency (such as weekly) for any tier. Priority is relative [P].
- Paid plans: Personal $19/mo or $209/yr for 2,000 images and 20 takedowns per month. Advanced $39/mo
  for 30,000 images and 300 takedowns. Pro $89/mo for 100,000 images and 900 takedowns [P, /monitor].
- Commission: 50% of the recovery. It is no-win, no-fee: submitting a case is free, and Pixsy charges
  only when payment is collected [P, pixsy.com/resolve].
- Cost if a case fails: none is stated [P, /resolve]. The Terms of Use say nothing about a fee when the
  photographer withdraws a case or settles directly [P, pixsy.com/terms-of-use; no in-force date shown].
  Do not read that silence as "no fee". Ask support before withdrawing any submitted case [U].

**Gotchas**
- **Pixsy becomes your exclusive agent once you submit a case.** The ToS says "once you make a case
  submission to Pixsy, unless Pixsy consents otherwise, Pixsy shall act as the exclusive agent for the
  identified infringement." You cannot also run that use through Copytrack, or contact the infringer
  yourself [P].
- **Stock-licensed matches are the main risk.** Pixsy's guidance is to confirm that an image was
  never licensed through an agency before filing, because "major stock agencies do not fully report
  sales to photographers" [P, pixsy.com/image-licensing/clarifying-licensing-stock-agencies].
  Shutterstock, Adobe and iStock do not name their buyers, so most of the 735 images cannot be cleared
  this way. Agencies can also license a use after the fact once their customer is contacted [H].
  In practice, sending a claim to a legitimate licensee is the most likely mistake.
- **Free pools make an image unenforceable [U, inference].** Once an image is downloadable free
  anywhere (Vecteezy Free, any CC or free tier), any use of it can be defended as licensed. This
  applies to the whole portfolio. Keep every image out of every free pool.
- **Whitelisting:** Pixsy has no named "approved domains" list. It offers domain-level **Ignore**
  (hides a domain from future match reports), **Approved Use** (for licensed uses) and **Not my
  image** [H, Pixsy help content seen through search]. Ignore these domains on day one:
  stock.adobe.com, shutterstock.com, istockphoto.com, gettyimages.*, alamy.*, dreamstime.com,
  123rf.com, fineartamerica.com, pixels.com, plus vecteezy.com, freepik.com / magnific.com,
  zazzle.com and picfair.com if you use those channels.
- **US registration:** you can submit a case without it. It is required to sue in the US, and it
  sets whether statutory damages are available [P, pixsy.com/register]. Under 17 U.S.C. §412,
  statutory damages and attorney's fees need registration before the infringement began, or within
  3 months of first publication. Uploading to a stock agency counts as publication (see
  `legal-ops.md`).
- **The USCO fee rises in mid-November 2026.** The group photo registration fee goes from $55 to $85
  if Congress does not intervene in its 120-day review. That review started 2026-07-14
  [3, fstoppers.com; USCO fee study at copyright.gov/rulemaking/feestudy2026]. If the 735 images
  are not yet registered, file GRPPH applications (up to 750 photos each, grouped by the calendar
  year of publication) before then.
- Subscriptions auto-renew. Cancel at least 5 business days before renewal [P]. Disputes fall under
  California law. The ToS has no arbitration clause [P].

**Owner vs automatable**
- Owner: the account (done), card details for any paid tier, and every case submission. A case
  appoints Pixsy as your agent and is a legal act, so the owner makes each one.
  Any USCO filing is also the owner's.
- Automatable after login: choosing which 500 images to monitor (favour images that are FAA-only or
  unusually distinctive), bulk upload, applying domain Ignores, and a weekly export of matches
  triaged against the agency sales CSVs.

**Copytrack.** No primary terms could be read: copytrack.com/pricing, /service,
/copytrack-terms-conditions/gtc-legal-claims and help.copytrack.com all returned 404 today. The
site now reads as infringer-facing. Secondary sources say photographers receive 55% of recoveries
(Copytrack keeps 45%), tracking is free under about 500 images, and there is no fee on a lost case
[3]. One also reports that the company renamed itself RD Legal GmbH in June 2025 [3, picdefense.io,
a defence-side source]. **Skip.** It adds a second agent on the same matches and brings a
duplicate-claim risk, and its terms cannot be verified.

Sources: [pixsy.com/monitor](https://www.pixsy.com/monitor) · [pixsy.com/resolve](https://www.pixsy.com/resolve) · [pixsy.com/terms-of-use](https://www.pixsy.com/terms-of-use) · [pixsy.com/register](https://www.pixsy.com/register) · [Pixsy stock-agency guidance](https://www.pixsy.com/image-licensing/clarifying-licensing-stock-agencies) · [Fstoppers on USCO fee](https://fstoppers.com/legal/what-upcoming-copyright-office-fee-increase-means-photographers-903585) · [USCO fee study 2026](https://www.copyright.gov/rulemaking/feestudy2026/) · [PicDefense on Copytrack](https://picdefense.io/demand-letters/enforcement/copytrack/)

## 2. Zazzle

**Verdict: go, with caveats.** It costs nothing. Landscape and architecture work suits the
calendar, poster and card products. Hold royalties at 10%, and treat Zazzle as print-on-demand
reach rather than a source of income.

**Key numbers** (Creator License Agreement and User Agreement, both effective 2025-04-01 [P])
- **Royalty:** you set it per account (default) or per product. Store-level defaults are not
  supported [P, Creator FAQ 2025-03-31]. The maximum is 50% for physical products and 99% for
  digital. Royalty is a markup, so a higher rate raises the retail price [P, FAQ].
  - Above 10%, an **Excess Royalty Fee** of 5% of gross royalty applies. For example, 14.9% on a
    $100 item pays about $14.16 [P].
  - A **Marketing Royalty Fee** is deducted whenever Zazzle's own marketing, an affiliate or another
    Ambassador drove the sale. The rate varies by product department. You cannot opt out, and the
    fee is not itemised [P, FAQ]. The department rate table is on a help page that returned 403.
    Community posts cite 35–50% of gross royalty [U]. Self-promotion links pay 35–50% of net sales
    in total, depending on department [P, Ambassador FAQ 2025-04-17].
  - "10% default" is widely assumed but was not confirmed in a primary source [U]. The minimum
    ("Standard Royalty Rate") was not confirmed either [U].
- **The royalty base is the discounted price.** Net Royalty Sales means "the purchase price received
  by Zazzle", less shipping, tax, refunds and upcharges [P, CLA §3.2.1.1]. Zazzle's near-constant
  sitewide sales therefore reduce your royalty.
- **Payout:** thresholds are $50 by PayPal or $100 by check, paid within 45 days after month end.
  Paying out below the threshold costs a $2.50 (PayPal) or $5 (check) fee [P, User Agreement].
- **Tax:** submit a W-9 in the account. Without a W-9 or W-8BEN, Zazzle may withhold at higher rates.
  You receive a 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC at the IRS thresholds, mailed at the end of January [P, Zazzle
  tax-forms help]. The PayPal 1099-K interaction is not documented [U].
- **Store:** the official seller guide has you open a store (free) as step 2. Every product you post
  for sale goes through content review first [P, "How to start selling"]. Earnings are paid "only if
  and when Zazzle determines" the account has complied for the whole period [P, User Agreement §23.1].
- **Public vs hidden products:** royalties accrue on any sale of a product that uses your content.
  The CLA does not distinguish public from private products [P]. Public products appear in
  marketplace search, where Marketing Royalty Fees are most likely to apply [U, inference].

**2027 calendar specs.** Zazzle does not publish pixel dimensions; the editor shows the print area.
- Sizes, as listed on the product page [P]: Small 5.5" × 7", Medium 8.5" × 11", Large 11" × 14.25".
  Layouts are two-page (photo above, grid below) or single-page. Wire binding, full-bleed printing,
  and a configurable start month and length.
- The resolution target for calendars is 200 ppi [3, Zazzle's image-size guide as cited in its
  community forum]. The bleed is reported as 1/16" beyond the trim, with a 1/8" safe zone [U].
- Derived targets per photo page, assuming a landscape page (confirm orientation in the editor):

| Size | Page | @200 ppi | @300 ppi | @300 ppi + 1/16" bleed each side |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 7 × 5.5 in | 1400 × 1100 | 2100 × 1650 | 2138 × 1688 |
| Medium | 11 × 8.5 in | 2200 × 1700 | 3300 × 2550 | 3338 × 2588 |
| Large | 14.25 × 11 in | 2850 × 2200 | 4275 × 3300 | 4313 × 3338 |

  Any 24 MP or larger original clears Large at 300 ppi. Export at the Large+bleed size, then let
  Zazzle scale down for the smaller sizes.

**Gotchas**
- **Leave digital downloads off.** They are off unless you enable them. When enabled, the buyer gets
  the file under a personal-use licence [P, FAQ]. That puts a full-resolution file in circulation,
  which weakens the stock versions of the same image.
- Content licence: non-exclusive, worldwide, sublicensable and transferable. Zazzle says it will not
  create AI derivatives of your content, but "may use Content to inform the development of new
  features" [P, FAQ].
- **Automation is restricted.** The FAQ says automated scraping tools and bots "are prohibited" and
  may lead to termination or withheld earnings [P]. No public creator upload API was found [U].
  Browser-driven bulk product creation carries a real risk to the account.
- Disputes go to binding arbitration (NAM or AAA, San Francisco), with a class-action waiver [P].
- After removal, content stays licensed for past orders and promotional use [P, FAQ].

**Owner vs automatable**
- Owner: W-9, PayPal payout email, the store name and URL, accepting agreements, the royalty
  default, and the first calendar product, to confirm the specs above in the editor.
- Automatable: exporting calendar-ready files to the table sizes, writing titles and tags from
  IPTC, and a product-copy checklist. Creating products in the Zazzle UI should stay hand-paced,
  given the bot clause.

Sources: [Creator License Agreement](https://www.zazzle.com/terms/updated_creator_license_agreement) · [User Agreement](https://www.zazzle.com/terms/user_agreement) · [Creator FAQ PDF (2025-03-31)](https://www.zazzle.com/assets/graphics/z5/zmisc/ambassador_program/Zazzle_Creator_FAQs.pdf) · [Ambassador FAQ PDF (2025-04-17)](https://www.zazzle.com/assets/graphics/z5/zmisc/ambassador_program/Ambassador_Program_FAQs.pdf) · [Tax forms help](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/tax_forms_for_u_s_citizens_and_international_creators_ambassadors-360004928233) · [How to start selling](https://www.zazzle.com/hc/article/how_to_start_selling_on_zazzle_a_step_by_step_guide-115003939948) · [Calendar product page](https://www.zazzle.com/2026_family_custom_photo_calendar-158317441261217323)

## 3. Picfair

**Verdict: skip for now.** This corrects `platform-matrix.md`, which credits Picfair with "its own
marketplace demand". That is no longer true. Picfair "does not include [a] searchable central
marketplace", and every store is a standalone site that you market yourself [P, support article
edited 2026-07-08]. It is a paid storefront and brings no buyers of its own. Revisit only if you
have your own traffic to send there, which would also answer the "own site" question in the matrix.

**Key numbers** (Picfair Support Centre, articles edited June–July 2026 [P])
- **Lite (free):** up to 20 images. The store stays unpublished, and "your images won't be publicly
  visible or offered for sale until your store is published as part of a Plus plan." **You cannot
  sell on the free plan.**
- **Plus:** monthly or annual billing in 13 currencies. The price is only on picfair.com/plans,
  which is behind a Cloudflare challenge and was not read [U]. An earlier internal note says about
  $11/mo. Picfair's blog promotes a 50%-off code, UPGRADE-50 [H].
  Plus includes up to 10,000 uploads (contact support to go higher), a custom domain, watermarks
  and priority moderation.
- **Commission: none on Plus sales.** "The price the photographer sets is what the customer pays
  and what the photographer receives" [P]. The old model, where the buyer paid a 20% markup, is gone.
- **Pricing:** you set one licence price per image, in bulk or individually. The minimum is £1.
  Prints cost the image price plus the partner's production and shipping. You cannot price print
  sizes separately. An optional **Custom Print Commission** (a percentage added only on print
  products) is available on Plus [P].
- **Licences:** Personal, Editorial and Commercial. Commercial is single-use (one story or
  campaign), unlimited in time and territory, and excludes paid ad slots and merchandise/resale.
  Moderation assigns licences, and you can change them [P].
- **Print fulfilment:** a global print-on-demand partner prints and ships. Prints are **on by default
  for the whole portfolio**, and opting out is possible only portfolio-wide, by contacting support [P].
- **Upload specs:** JPEG or HEIC only, at least 800 px on each side, under 50 MB. Upload is by
  drag-and-drop in the web library, with optional AI "Smart Captions". No FTP, CSV or Lightroom
  plugin was documented [P]. Moderation takes about 1 business day.
- **Payout:** you cash out per sale by email. Payment is in GBP by PayPal (or Wise; bank transfer is
  UK-only), averaging 14 days and taking up to 1 month [P].
- **Exclusivity:** non-exclusive [P].

**Gotchas:** you pay a subscription with no platform demand behind it. Your JPEG masters must stay
under 50 MB. Payment in GBP adds FX cost. Prints are on by default, which competes with FAA pricing
for the same images.

**Owner vs automatable:** the Plus subscription and payment, publishing the store, and pricing
decisions are the owner's. Uploads are browser-only and could be driven through Chrome. Titles and
tags can come from embedded IPTC or from Smart Captions.

Sources: [Store cost](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417585339793) · [Upload limits](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4471249646097) · [Print settings](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417629272977) · [Custom print commission](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417584131345) · [No central marketplace](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/14684166812061) · [Image requirements](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417575509393) · [Moderation](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417577654801) · [Payouts](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417631067793) · [Exclusivity](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417562383505) · [Commercial licence](https://support.picfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417555749137)

## 4. Vecteezy (contributor)

**Verdict: go, on the Pro licence only.** It has the cleanest terms of the new stock channels.

**Key numbers** (Contributor Agreement dated 2025-08-04; help center [P])
- **Application:** create an account under your legal name and date of birth as shown on government
  ID, with an address and portfolio links. Accept the Contributor Agreement, upload **25** files that
  meet the Content Guidelines, and complete ID verification. Review takes about 5–7 days. A low
  approval rate on the first 25 "may disqualify you" [P]. (The landing page says 10 files [H]. The
  help article, updated 2025-12-02, says 25. Plan for 25.)
- **ID verification:** handled by Persona, using a government ID plus a live selfie by webcam or
  phone. It is mandatory, since only verified contributors are reviewed [P].
- **Royalty:** **Pro** pays a 50/50 split under the Subscriber Share model: (the customer's revenue
  less card fees and refunds) × 50% × your share of that customer's downloads [P, updated
  2026-05-11]. **Free** pays $5 per 1,000 downloads [P].
  "Net Revenue" in the agreement deducts broad "fixed expenses": tax, refunds, affiliate costs,
  third-party service costs and content-acquisition costs [P].
- **Payout:** you set a threshold between $25 and $2,000. Payment is by PayPal or Payoneer, about
  45–60 days after the month the threshold is reached. Tax information must be on file [P].
- **Exclusivity:** non-exclusive by default. Exclusive status is opt-in and requires removing the
  files from all other sites [P, §5].
- **Min specs:** 4–50 MP, 50 MB or less, JPG at maximum quality, sRGB. Titles and keywords must be
  unique and in English. Editorial titles must follow the format "City, State, Country, Year -
  description" [P, photo guidelines].
- **Bulk:** FTP is approved in the agreement (§3e). CSV metadata upload uses the columns Filename,
  Title, Description, Keywords and License. The template can be downloaded from Add Data, and FTP
  plus CSV are supported [P]. Auto-Submit and "Magic Metadata" are available [P].
- **AI training:** there is **no contributor opt-out**, and the agreement does not mention it
  explicitly. The agreement authorises sublicensing to "Partners" for "any reasonable business
  purpose ... to develop new features and products". Compensation for Partner deals is at
  Vecteezy's "sole discretion" [P, §3a and §12]. Separately, Vecteezy sells a **Data Training
  Licence** as an add-on to Enterprise licences, and standard licences prohibit training [P,
  licensing agreement updated 2026-06-22]. Expect your Pro files to be eligible for data licensing,
  with payment on terms Vecteezy decides.

**Gotchas:** the Free pool destroys enforceability (see Pixsy). Liability is capped at $1,000 [P].
Kentucky law and Kentucky courts govern [P]. Termination takes 30 days' notice. Files stay available
for 60 days in buyers' download histories, and longer on Partner sites [P, §3d and §13].

**Owner vs automatable:** account creation, legal name, date of birth, ID and selfie through
Persona, the agreement, and tax and payout setup are the owner's. After approval, FTP upload plus
CSV built from existing IPTC is fully scriptable. It follows the same pattern as
`scripts/stock-ftp-upload.py`.

Sources: [Become a contributor](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025609751) · [ID verification](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402678669204) · [PPD Pro earnings](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025811171) · [PPD Free vs Pro](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024753291) · [Payment threshold](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/27469902019988) · [CSV upload](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029388091) · [Photo guidelines](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037454312) · [Contributor Agreement (PDF, 2025-08-04)](https://eezycontributors.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20574579753236) · [Licensing agreement](https://www.vecteezy.com/licensing-agreement) · [Contributors page](https://www.vecteezy.com/contributors)

## 5. Magnific (formerly Freepik) contributor

**Verdict: go, with caveats, as a low-priority tail channel.** Earnings per download are low, and
the company sells its archive for AI training.

Primary sources were **blocked**. contributor.magnific.com/terms, magnific.com/legal/* and the
redirected support.freepik.com articles all returned an Akamai bot challenge or a 403. No attempt was
made to get past the challenge. Items marked [H] or [3] need confirming in the dashboard after
approval.

**Key numbers**
- **Rebrand:** the company became Magnific on 2026-04-28. The contributor program kept its terms
  [3, photokeyworder.ai 2026-06-29; consistent with `platform-matrix.md`].
- **Application:** a single-format qualification test on web upload. Reported sizes conflict:
  150–200 files [3, photokeyworder.ai] versus 20 images at Level 1 [3, xpiksapp.com 2026-09-07].
  If rejected, you can reapply after 1 month [3]. **Check the dashboard before preparing the batch.**
- **Levels:** Level 2 allows about 200 web uploads per month. Level 3 unlocks FTP or SFTP. Reported
  thresholds vary by source [3].
- **Royalty:** 50% of net subscriber revenue, allocated per download: (net revenue from the
  subscriber ÷ that subscriber's downloads) × 50% [H, support.freepik.com snippet]. Reported rates
  are about $0.04–0.07 per download [3]. Contributor content is **Premium-only**, so contributors
  cannot put it in the free tier [H/3]. That is good for enforceability.
- **Payout:** $25 by PayPal or $50 by Payoneer (SEPA is EU-only). Payment covers downloads from
  2 months earlier [H].
- **Tax:** Freepik Company S.L.U. is in Spain. Contributors report **24% Spanish withholding
  without a tax-residency certificate** [3]. Getting a US certificate means IRS Form 8802 for a
  Form 6166, which carries a user fee. The treaty royalty rate is not confirmed [U].
- **Min specs:** photos 2,000–10,000 px, 4–100 MP, 1.5–250 MB, JPG, RGB [3].
- **Bulk:** FTP or SFTP from Level 3. The CSV is **semicolon-delimited** with comma-separated
  keywords, in the columns File name; Title; Keywords; Prompt; Model [3].
- **Exclusivity:** the contributor licence is non-exclusive, worldwide and transferable, and covers
  "transform, make derivative works of" [3, quoted from the licence terms].

**AI training**
- In April 2024, Freepik told Reuters it had licensed "the majority of its archive of 200 million
  images" to two large tech firms at about 2–4¢ per image, with more deals in progress [3,
  PetaPixel citing Reuters].
- F Lite, its own model, was trained on about 80M images from Freepik's "internal dataset" [3,
  TechCrunch 2025-04-29].
- No contributor opt-out or contributor share for AI deals was found [U]. The "never trains on your
  data" claim applies to **users of the AI suite**, not to contributor uploads [3].
- EyeEm (a Freepik subsidiary, now closed) licensed contributor photos for AI with an opt-out and a
  stated 50/50 share [3]. That does not show Freepik contributors get the same deal.

**Gotchas:** you must accept that your uploads may go into AI data deals without a confirmed
payment. The Spanish withholding needs sorting out. The monthly upload caps slow the rollout of
735 images to about 4 months or more.

**Owner vs automatable:** the application, identity details, tax declaration (W-8BEN-E or residency
certificate) and payout account are the owner's. After Level 3, FTP plus semicolon CSV from IPTC is
scriptable. Level 1–2 web uploads can be driven through Chrome within the monthly caps.

Sources: [photokeyworder.ai guide (2026-06-29)](https://blog.photokeyworder.ai/2026/06/29/the-complete-magnific-ex-freepik-contributor-guide-requirements-csv-metadata-ai-images-and-best-practices/) · [Xpiks review (2026-09-07)](https://xpiksapp.com/blog/freepik-magnific-review/) · [Freepik pay-per-download (redirects, blocked)](https://support.freepik.com/s/article/Pay-per-download-and-RPI?language=en_US) · [PetaPixel AI deals](https://petapixel.com/2024/04/11/all-the-photo-companies-that-have-struck-licensing-deals-with-ai-firms/) · [TechCrunch F Lite](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/29/freepik-releases-an-open-ai-image-generator-trained-on-licensed-data) · [AutoKeyWorder guide](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/freepik-contributor-guide/)

was found that pays individual photographers for an existing, non-exclusively licensed landscape
library.
