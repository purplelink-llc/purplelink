ModernPaper

A PDF reader and editor for people who read papers for a living. It annotates, searches across your whole library, reads scans with OCR, fills forms and redacts, and it all runs on your Mac with no account.

Try it free for 7 days

The trial is the complete app for seven days from the first time you open it, with no account and nothing to enter. Buying is a single $49 payment. Your license key is on the page after you pay and in your receipt email. Not useful? Email ben@purplelink.llc within 14 days of purchase for a refund.

Also in the Mac Suite: ModernTex, Outbound Veil, Legroom, Keyfeel, Tapefolio, Panely, Summonly, Née, Lenses, ModernPaper and Vitae Plus for life, $99.99 once.

What it does

Read and mark up

Highlights, notes and drawings, with a Read mode that can read the page aloud with a system voice.

Find things

A search index across your PDFs, and OCR so scanned articles search like any other file.

Fix the file

Fill forms, rearrange pages, and redact text so it is removed from the file, not just covered.

Price and trial

Seven days free

The trial counts from the first time you open the app. No account and nothing to enter.

What stays free

After the trial, reading, searching and read-aloud keep working. Saving annotations, forms, page tools, redaction and the Claude panel need a license key.

$49, once

One payment in US dollars, through Stripe. The key arrives by email and on the page after you pay, and works offline.

The Claude panel is optional

If you want to ask questions about a paper, you can add your own Anthropic API key. The key is kept in your Mac's Keychain, and what you ask about goes straight from your Mac to Anthropic, not through Purplelink. Anthropic bills you for that use. Without a key the panel is off and nothing is sent.

System requirements

MacmacOS 14.0 or later.
InternetNot needed to read, search or annotate. Used only by the optional Claude panel.
