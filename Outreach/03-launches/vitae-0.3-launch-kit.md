## A. LinkedIn, personal (about 1,200 characters)

> Vitae 0.3 is out. Vitae is a free macOS app that keeps an academic record in one place (papers, submissions, grants, teaching, service) and builds the CV from it.
>
> This release reads your mail. Connect iCloud, Gmail, Outlook or a university mailbox, and Vitae checks for new mail every 15 minutes. Decision letters, deadlines and review invitations go to an Inbox for review. Applying a decision files the letter with that review round. Nothing changes without a click, and messages stay unread.
>
> Three other additions:
> (1) Daily backups: a snapshot every day Vitae opens, and undo for every change.
> (2) Shareables: images sized for LinkedIn, Instagram and X when a paper is accepted or a grant is funded.
> (3) Vitae Plus: an optional $3 a month tier for reports (NIH biosketch, Current and Pending), Word templates and a few extras. Record keeping stays free.
>
> The library stays on your Mac. There is no account.
>
> Download: purplelink.llc/vitae
>
> #AcademicLife #PhD #HigherEd #macOS

## Notes
