## Ranked shortlist (for Ben)

1. TUG "TeX Resources on the Web" (webmaster@tug.org): best fit; the page has a commercial section and invites additions.
2. OnTheHub "Sell With Us" form: only verified vendor intake among academic resellers.
3. MacUpdate "Add App" form (account needed) plus support@macupdate.com for questions.
4. awesome-mac pull request (paid apps are accepted there).
5. AMS vendors list (tech-support@ams.org), the TeX FAQ GitHub issue, MacUpdate content team pitch, AlternativeTo (community-driven).
6. Student Beans, Softpedia, Mac Informer: low value.

Those pages were read with curl or the built-in browser pane instead; all text and addresses above come from the fetched page source.
