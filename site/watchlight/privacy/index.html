Watchlight Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · ben@purplelink.llc

Watchlight is an iPhone app from Purplelink LLC. It checks messages, links and calls for signs of scams and tells you what it found. Every check runs on your iPhone. Watchlight has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it contains no code from other companies. Nothing is sent to Purplelink. A few things leave your phone only when you send them, and each is listed below. The policy for this website is the Purplelink privacy policy. The Watchlight Terms of Use explain what the app can and cannot do.

What Watchlight looks at

Texts from unknown senders, once you turn on the filter in Messages (the filter button at the top left, then Manage Filtering, then Text Message Filter, then Watchlight). iOS hands Watchlight only texts from numbers that are not in your contacts, never your iMessage conversations with contacts. For each text, Watchlight tells iOS one thing: Junk, Transactions, Promotions, allow, or nothing. Apple's filter system can forward texts to a company's server for checking; Watchlight does not use that option.

Anything you choose to check: a pasted message, a text, link or email you share to Watchlight, a QR code, or a screenshot you pick.

Calls: Watchlight gives iOS a list of phone numbers that many people reported to the FTC, so your iPhone can show a label when one of them calls. Watchlight never sees your calls.

Web pages in Safari, if you turn on the Safari extension: it reads the page's address and title and whether the page asks for a password, so it can warn you about fake sign-in pages. It reads nothing you type and sends nothing.

What stays on your iPhone

Counts of the messages you checked, by kind, for the stats screen (numbers only). Your settings: the campaign-text setting and, if you bought the unlock and chose one, your trusted person's first name and one phone number or email. A small diagnostic log in the app's own storage, capped in size, which records which rules matched, the scores, the verdict and the message length in characters, never the message or the sender.

Screenshots, QR codes, emails and messages you check are not saved. Deleting Watchlight deletes all of the above.

When something leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Report (swipe left on a text or call in Messages or Phone, then Report): iOS sends the text or call details and its sender to your carrier's 7726 spam-reporting service.
  • Report it to 7726 opens a draft in Messages with the text. You decide whether to send it.
  • Ask someone I trust and Text [your trusted person] open the share sheet or a draft in Messages or Mail with the message and Watchlight's verdict. You pick who gets it, and you send it.
  • Buying the unlock goes through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Watchlight learns only that the unlock is owned. If you use Family Sharing, members of your family group can use it too. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Links in the app (sources, the refund page, the support email, and breach and data-broker sites) open in Safari or Mail. Those sites have their own policies.

If you turned on Share with App Developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports with us. TestFlight testers can send us feedback. Both come through Apple and never include your messages unless you attach them.

Camera, photos and contacts

Camera: used only to read the link inside a QR code. Nothing is recorded. Photos: you pick a screenshot through Apple's photo picker; Watchlight gets only that image, reads its text on the iPhone, and does not keep it. Contacts: if you choose a trusted person, Apple's contact picker gives Watchlight only the one phone number or email you tap. Watchlight never reads your contacts.

The on-device AI check

For screenshots, Watchlight can ask Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) for a second opinion. It runs on your iPhone and sends nothing.

Children

Watchlight is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because Purplelink holds no personal data from Watchlight, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you want to ask about this policy, email ben@purplelink.llc. We do not sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Watchlight changes what it stores or sends, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released, and update the App Privacy details in the App Store to match.

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected. Watchlight does its checks on your iPhone and sends nothing to us. Reports, messages and purchases you start go to 7726, the people you choose, or Apple.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ben@purplelink.llc.
