Vitae Plus

Vitae is free. Plus is an optional $5 upgrade that adds a few extras for fun: color themes, alternate Dock icons, a small celebration when good news arrives, and a case for your milestones. Buying it supports Vitae's development.

Download Vitae, free

One payment, no subscription. Stripe handles the card; Purplelink never sees it. After checkout you get a license key to paste into Vitae. No account, no sign-in.

What Plus adds

Four extras, none of them essential

Color themes

Alternate color themes for the app, for when the default look has worn thin.

Alternate Dock icons

A choice of other icons for Vitae in the Dock.

Celebrations

A burst of confetti when you mark a paper accepted or a grant awarded.

Milestones case

A trophy case that collects milestones from your own record.

What stays free

Everything that keeps your record

Submissions and review rounds, grants, projects, collaborators, teaching, service and mentoring, the tenure view, inbox triage, CV and package exports, and updates are all free, with no limits on how much you keep. Plus does not unlock any of them, and not buying it takes nothing away.

The license

Checked on your Mac

Buy once

Checkout runs on Stripe. When it finishes, this site shows your license key.

Paste the key

In Vitae, open Settings, then Vitae Plus. Paste the key and press Activate.

No account, no server check

Vitae verifies the key's signature on your Mac and never contacts a server to check it. The key holds a date and a short order reference, not your name or email.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Vitae Plus to use Vitae?
No. Vitae is free, with every record-keeping feature and no limits. Plus adds cosmetic and fun extras only.
Is it a subscription?
No. It is $5, paid once.
Does Vitae contact a server to check the license?
No. Vitae checks the key's signature on your Mac. There is no account and no sign-in. See the privacy policy for what the purchase itself involves.
What if I lose my key?
Your Stripe receipt has the order number. Email ben@purplelink.llc with it and you will get the key again.
