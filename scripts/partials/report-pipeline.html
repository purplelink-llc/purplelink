What each check does, and what one chat prompt leaves out

Paper Review

  • References. Each reference is matched to a CrossRef record, and retractions, withdrawals and expressions of concern are flagged. Up to 40 key claims are checked against the cited source's abstract, fetched from OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar or CrossRef.
  • Rules, no AI. p-values are recomputed from t, F, chi-square, r and z (statcheck), and reported means are tested for consistency (GRIM). In-text citations are matched to the reference list, and figure and table numbering is checked.
  • Vision. The first 30 pages are rendered and read as images. Figures are compared with what the text claims, including missing error bars, broken axes and color-only encoding.
  • Seven reviewers. Methodology, statistics, data integrity, the editor's view, equations, literature and numerical realism each read the paper separately.
  • Reconcile. Findings are merged, and issues raised by more than one reviewer rank first. A coherence check compares the abstract, hypotheses and results with each other.
  • Deliver. A report with a fix-first checklist and an annotated PDF arrives by email. The manuscript is deleted within about a day of your first opening the result, or within 48 hours if you never do.

A chat prompt

  • References are not looked up one by one, so a wrong or invented one can pass.
  • Retractions are caught only if the model happens to know them.
  • p-values and means are not recomputed.
  • One point of view, written in one go.
  • No marked-up PDF.
  • Whether the manuscript is kept or used for training depends on your account settings.

The reviewers and the final pass use Claude Opus through Anthropic's API, which does not train on your manuscript.
