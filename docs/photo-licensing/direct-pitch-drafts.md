# Direct licensing pitches: drafts (not sent)

Drafted 2026-09-24. **Nothing here has been sent.** Target list, filenames, contact sources and notes are in `direct-pitch-targets.csv`: ranks 1-15 have drafts below, and ranks 16-23 are alternates with no draft.

## Rules for every pitch

- **Non-exclusive only.** Every image here is already live on stock sites and on Fine Art America. Never offer or imply exclusivity (see `art-consultant-package.md` §1).
- **No model or property releases are offered.** The sets were filtered to leave out portraits, identifiable people, sports, parties and protests. Some street scenes still show incidental passers-by, so check before sending.
- **Artwork and trademark caveats.** The glass sculpture at Atlanta Botanical Garden, the Rijksmuseum library interior and the Godzilla head at Hotel Gracery all involve third-party rights. These are flagged in the CSV notes.
- **Confirm before sending:** the Bowfin identification (the metadata never names the sub), the Jungfraujoch vantage points, and whether each Tucson "desert city" frame shows Tucson.
- **Blue Lagoon** requires a film application for commercial photography on site. Check this against the commercial stock listings for DSC_0748/0755 before pitching.
- **Don't trust the auto-generated captions without opening the file.** Found 2026-09-25: DSC_7181 was captioned as Hotel d'Angleterre's facade and used in that pitch, but the image is an unrelated Copenhagen street with heart-shaped ornaments — no hotel in frame. DSC_7200, unrelated to any target here, was captioned as a stone cairn on a tree stump, confirming this isn't a one-off: something in the captioning pass mismatched files to descriptions across the library. Open every file before it goes out, not just the caption. (The Hotel d'Angleterre entry has been corrected to DSC_7189, verified by eye.) None of the other filenames in this doc or in `direct-pitch-targets.csv` have been re-verified against their actual images yet.

## Pricing basis

Published photographer pricing guides put a 1-2 year non-exclusive web and organic-social license for a 10-20 image set at about $500-2,000, with advertising use priced higher ([Raised Media Co.](https://www.raisedmediaco.com/the-dailies/what-are-commercial-licensing-fees-photo-video), [Patrick Fore](https://www.patrickfore.com/blog/commercial-photography-licensing-fees-exploration)). The drafts quote $150-350 per image for businesses and a pick-10 bundle of about $1,000-1,200 for tourism boards, which usually already run free image banks. The license scope is web, social and email marketing. Print advertising is quoted separately.
