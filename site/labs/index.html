Paper Review plans: a monthly allowance for the group

A plan gives the lab a set number of Paper Reviews each month, with the Revision Review and Response Review for each of those papers included. Both plans can be paid monthly or yearly. Yearly billing gives two months free.

Lab plans are not on sale yet. To be told when they open, or to set one up by invoice now, email ben@purplelink.llc.

What every plan includes

  • The full Paper Review for each paper, with the journal fit check and the anonymity check, the same as a pack review.
  • Reviews arrive in monthly batches, on yearly billing too. Each month's reviews can be used until the end of the following month, then they expire.
  • One Revision Review and one Response Review for every paper reviewed on the plan, at no extra cost. The links arrive in the report email and on the plan page, and stay valid for 12 months. The response letter draft is not included; it is sold separately on the Response Review page.
  • Each report is emailed to the address typed on the upload form, so every member gets their own report.
  • A plan page, reached by a link sent by email. It shows the reviews left and each month's links, turns renewal off or back on, and opens the billing page for the card and invoices.
  • Cancel any time. The plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for, and reviews already issued stay usable until their date. Yearly plans get a reminder email 30 days and 7 days before renewal.

Paying by card, invoice or purchase order

Prices are in US dollars.

Paying by purchase order or invoice
If your purchasing office must pay first, email ben@purplelink.llc with what you need and your billing address. A Stripe invoice follows within one business day, payable by card or US bank transfer. This covers packs, lab plans and ModernTex seats.

Where a manuscript goes

For a research office or ethics board, in brief. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Paper Review and the other paid checks
The manuscript is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, inputs are retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and are not used for model training. Reference titles and DOIs are sent to CrossRef. The Paper Review report is emailed as a PDF when the review finishes, through Resend, to the address typed on the upload form. The manuscript is not emailed. Purplelink deletes the manuscript and the online copy within about a day after the result is first opened, or within 48 hours if it never is.
Clinical and patient data
Purplelink is not a HIPAA business associate. Do not upload protected health information or identifiable participant data. De-identify first. Unpublished results are fine because the manuscript is deleted within about a day after you open the result, and Anthropic does not train on it.
