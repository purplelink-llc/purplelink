KDD reference format

KDD 2027 research track papers use the ACM template: acmart in sigconf layout with ACM-Reference-Format.bst. References come after the 8 content pages and do not count toward them at submission.

Checked 2026-10-01 against the sources linked at the end of this page.

What KDD expects

  • Reference style: ACM Reference Format, through the ACM template the call requires.
  • Class: \documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart} is the recommended setting in the call.
  • Length at submission: 8 content pages, followed by references and an optional appendix with no page limit.
  • Length at camera-ready: 12 pages in total, of which 9 are content pages. References and appendix together are limited to 3 pages.
  • Review: Double-blind. Refer to your own prior work in the third person.

Setting up the bibliography

The acmart package ships its own bibliography style, ACM-Reference-Format.bst. At the point where the reference list should appear:

\bibliographystyle{ACM-Reference-Format}
\bibliography{refs}

ACM's reference page says to use a BibTeX (.bib) file with the ACM Reference Format .bst, and that ACM prefers full author names over initials. In-text citations are numbers in square brackets, such as [1] or [1, 2].

Three references in ACM Reference Format

  1. Tianqi Chen and Carlos Guestrin. 2016. XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System. In Proceedings of the 22nd ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining. ACM, 785–794. https://doi.org/10.1145/2939672.2939785
  2. Aditya Grover and Jure Leskovec. 2016. node2vec: Scalable Feature Learning for Networks. In Proceedings of the 22nd ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining. ACM, 855–864. https://doi.org/10.1145/2939672.2939754
  3. Marco Tulio Ribeiro, Sameer Singh, and Carlos Guestrin. 2016. “Why Should I Trust You?”: Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier. In Proceedings of the 22nd ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining. ACM, 1135–1144. https://doi.org/10.1145/2939672.2939778

Produced by running ACM-Reference-Format.bst over a .bib file built from the Crossref record for each DOI. Titles, authors, volumes and pages were checked against Crossref on 2026-10-01.

LaTeX template

KDD uses the ACM Primary Article Template. Our ACM acmart template page has a compilable sigconf skeleton and explains the review and anonymous options.

Common mistakes

  • Planning the camera-ready around unlimited references. At submission they are unlimited, but the published paper allows 3 pages for references and appendix together.
  • Dropping the anonymous option, which leaves the author block visible in a double-blind submission.
  • Abbreviating first names. ACM's reference page states a preference for full names, and ACM-Reference-Format prints whatever the author field holds.
  • Leaving out the doi field. The style prints the DOI as a link when the field is present and prints nothing when it is absent, with no warning.

Frequently asked

What reference style does KDD use?

The ACM Reference Format. In LaTeX that is ACM-Reference-Format.bst, which ships with the acmart package.

Do references count toward the KDD page limit?

Not at submission: the KDD 2027 call allows 8 content pages followed by references and an optional appendix. The camera-ready is 12 pages in total with 9 content pages.

Which acmart options does KDD use?

The KDD 2027 research track call recommends \documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}.
