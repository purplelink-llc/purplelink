How to Check Your Thesis Formatting Before You Submit It

By Benjamin Ampel · October 8, 2026

Purplelink's free Thesis Format Checker reads a thesis or dissertation PDF in your browser. It checks margins, font size, page numbers, line spacing and front matter against your graduate school's rules and flags what needs a second look before you submit.

Steps

  1. Pull your graduate school's formatting guide for its minimums, then enter them at purplelink.llc/tools/thesis-format-checker/: a margin preset or custom inches per side, page size, minimum font size and a line spacing requirement.

  2. Click Choose a PDF or drop your thesis into the box. Files up to 200 MB are accepted. A long document takes a minute or two to read.

  3. Read the summary line once the report loads: page count, and how many checks passed, need review, or are informational.

  4. Open the page size, margins, page numbers, fonts and line spacing cards. Each card names the exact pages that are off instead of giving only a pass or fail.

  5. Check the front matter card. It lists, in order, which standard headings it found in the first 40 pages (title or approval page, copyright, abstract, table of contents and more). That confirms a heading exists. It does not confirm that your university accepts its placement.

  6. Click Download this report as text and save it with your draft, as a record for the next revision.

  7. Fix only the pages a Review flag names, and only if the page is wrong. Wide tables, landscape figures and chapter openings without numbers are often fine as they are.

What happens under the hood

The checker reads your PDF with pdf.js (a PDF reader library), which runs in your browser. The file is never uploaded, not even to Purplelink's servers. The checker measures where the text layer sits on each page for margins, reads font metadata for size and embedding, and reads printed page numbers for sequence. Front matter detection matches short lines against common heading patterns in the first 40 pages and skips anything shaped like a table-of-contents entry. A scanned thesis has no text layer, so the checker has nothing to measure.

Q&A

Will this tell me whether my university will accept my thesis?

No. It measures your document against the numbers you enter, so pull those numbers from your own graduate school's formatting guide first. Rules differ between universities and change over time.

What does a Review status mean?

There is no Fail status. Review means a page measured outside what you entered. That is often fine (a wide table or a chapter opening without a number), so look at the page yourself before changing anything.
