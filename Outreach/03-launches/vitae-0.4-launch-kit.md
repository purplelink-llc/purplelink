Why it is written this way:
- **Search keywords in the first two lines:** academic CV, Word or PDF, papers, grants, macOS. Those lines show before "…see more" and are what LinkedIn search indexes most.
- **Short paragraphs and a numbered list,** so it can be skimmed on a phone.
- **A closing question** that asks for real input (missing CV formats) rather than generic engagement bait.
- **Five hashtags,** chosen to be specific: #AcademicCV and #Zotero have focused audiences, while #PhD and #HigherEd bring reach.

## A. LinkedIn, personal (1,568 characters)

> Vitae 0.4 imports your academic CV. Give it a Word or PDF CV, and it reads your papers, grants, talks, teaching, service and mentoring into one library on your Mac.
>
> Vitae is a free macOS app for faculty and PhD students. It keeps the academic record (submissions, review rounds, grants, teaching, service) and builds the CV, annual review and tenure packet from it.
>
> This release adds three features:
>
> (1) CV import. Vitae reads Word, PDF, RTF and text CVs in the common citation styles (APA, Chicago, MLA, IEEE, ACM, Harvard, Vancouver). Nothing is saved until you review it, and entries already in your library are flagged.
>
> (2) Seven CV formats. One library produces a full academic CV, a short CV, a one-page CV, an industry résumé, and APA, IEEE and teaching-focused versions. Export Several Formats writes them to a folder in one step.
>
> (3) Send to Zotero. Vitae adds your publications to your Zotero library, or fills in missing fields, after showing every change. It never deletes anything in Zotero.
>
> The importer was tested on 35 CV files (public CVs from economics, sociology and information systems, plus synthetic CVs in ten citation styles). Field accuracy (title, venue, year, volume, pages, status) was about 95 percent or higher on every file.
>
> Your library stays on your Mac. There is no account, and record keeping is free. Feedback and bug reports come straight to me through Help > Send Feedback in the app.
>
> Which CV format does your department or the job market ask for that is missing from this list?
>

## Notes

- Every number in the post is checked. The importer's test set was 35 CV files: Ben's own CV, public CVs and synthetic CVs. Field accuracy ran from 94.9 to 100 percent, which the post rounds to "about 95 percent or higher". Do not claim user counts or testimonials. None exist yet.
- CV formats: without Vitae Plus they export at full length with Vitae's citation style, and Word becomes Rich Text. If someone asks, per-format length, citation style and Word output are part of Plus.
- Privacy answer: CV files are read on the Mac and never uploaded. Send to Zotero talks only to the user's own Zotero account.
