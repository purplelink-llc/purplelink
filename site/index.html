ModernTex · LaTeX editor for Mac

A modern LaTeX editor that runs on your Mac

A native Mac app that builds your paper on your own machine. There is no account, no subscription and no compile limit, and it works offline. If you have no TeX installed, it sets up TinyTeX in one click. Errors come back in plain language, and a submission check flags problems before a journal does. Cite from Zotero while the PDF preview follows along.

Free for 7 days, then $19.99 once

See ModernTex

Outbound Veil · Privacy for Mac

Catches personal data before you hit send

It checks what you type for names, Social Security and card numbers, API keys and passwords, and offers to redact them. In the apps you choose, it holds the send key until you decide.

Free for 7 days, then $29.99

See Outbound Veil

Legroom · Free space for Mac

An automated disk cleaner

You approve each cleaning rule from a preview, and after that it runs on a schedule or when free space drops below your line. A log shows every run, and one switch pauses it. A Space Map and daily measurements show what grew over 1, 7 and 30 days.

Free for 7 days, then $9.99

See Legroom

Keyfeel · Keyboard sounds for Mac

Typing sounds recorded from real keyboards

Recordings of Tactile, Cherry MX Brown, Dome and IBM Model M switches, with click sounds and scroll ticks that follow your speed. It goes quiet while the microphone is in use.

Free for 7 days, then $9.99

See Keyfeel

Tapefolio · Meeting transcription for Mac

Record, transcribe and summarize your meetings on your Mac

It records Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and FaceTime calls without a bot joining, then transcribes them with speakers labeled and writes a draft summary with topics and times. It asks you to confirm that everyone agreed to be recorded. Names can be swapped for codes before you share. It misses some, and the summary can be wrong, so check both against the transcript. Summaries need a Mac with Apple Intelligence.

Free for 7 days, then $29.99

See Tapefolio

Panely · Window snapping for Mac

Snap windows at the press of a key

Press a shortcut to snap a window to halves, thirds, sixths or an area you define, or drag it to a screen edge. Panely then offers the free part of the screen to another window or app.

Free for 7 days, then $9.99

See Panely

Summonly · Search launcher for Mac

One search bar to find any file

Press Option-Space to search file names, the start of text files and PDFs, apps and the clipboard. It finds connected drives by name and understands filters such as kind:pdf and modified:week.

Free for 7 days, then $9.99

See Summonly

Née · File actions for Mac

Transform your files in two steps

Drag files in Finder while holding Option, then drop them on a slice of the ring that opens at your pointer: convert, compress, merge or transcribe. The result is saved beside the original, which is never changed.

Free for 7 days, then $14.99

See Née

Lenses · AR glasses setup for Mac

Get the most out of your AR glasses

Plug them in and your Mac sets itself up. It applies a profile when the glasses connect and restores your display when they leave: sharp text, 120 Hz where the glasses offer it, and a See-Through Mode for the room around your windows.

$4.99, no trial

See Lenses

Vitae · Academic record for Mac

Every paper, grant and deadline in one place

Vitae keeps your submissions, grants, projects and co-authors in one record on your Mac, with no account. Import a CV, DOIs, BibTeX or Zotero, check what it found, and generate your CV when you need it. The app is free; Plus adds Word export, one-page CVs and reports.

Free app, Plus from $2.99 a month

See Vitae

GlobePin · Travel globe for iPhone

Never forget a trip

Every place and flight goes on a 3D globe. Attach photos, notes and postcards to each place, get a reminder on the anniversary of a visit, and turn a trip or a whole year into a short video.

Free for iPhone and iPad

See GlobePin

haea · Health analytics for iPhone

Health analytics built on peer-reviewed research

haea turns sleep, heart rate, HRV, workouts and weight from Apple Health into a daily readiness briefing. Its methods draw on published research, and the analysis runs on your iPhone.

Coming to the App Store. Free, with an optional subscription

See haea
