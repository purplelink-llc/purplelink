Questions

What does “for life” mean for Vitae Plus?
The key you get is valid for 100 years and never asks to renew, and Plus stays unlocked for as long as Purplelink offers Vitae Plus. If Plus is ever discontinued, we will say so first, and the key keeps working in the version of Vitae you have.
Do I need the Suite to use Vitae?
No. Vitae is free, with all core record-keeping and no limits. Plus is the optional paid layer on top, and the Suite includes it for life.
Will I get future updates?
Yes, every 1.x update of ModernTex and of Outbound Veil, and Vitae updates as they come. A future 2.0 of an app may be sold separately; the Suite covers the 1.x line you bought.
I already own ModernTex or Outbound Veil
Email ben@purplelink.llc before you buy, and we will work out a fair credit for what you already paid.
Can I try them first?
Yes. ModernTex and Outbound Veil each have a free 7-day trial, and Vitae is free. After you buy, you install the full copy from your download page.
What is the refund policy?
Email ben@purplelink.llc within 14 days of purchase and we will refund the full $39 to the original payment method, with no reason needed. A refunded Suite's ModernTex license key and Vitae Plus key are no longer licensed for use.
Which Macs does it run on?
ModernTex and Outbound Veil need macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Vitae needs macOS 15 or later. There is no Windows or iPhone version.
Does Outbound Veil send my text anywhere?
No. The checking runs on your Mac, nothing you type is stored or sent, and the app has no analytics. Its page lists the apps it has been tested in.
