# Lenses: before and after shots through the glasses (for Ben to film)

Nothing in the launch video is footage through the glasses. These six pairs would replace the drawings. Film with a phone
held at the lens, same position and exposure for both halves of a pair, glasses on a RayNeo Air, Mac on macOS 14 or later.
Lock exposure and focus on the phone before the first shot. Keep each clip 6 to 10 seconds. Do not use a screen recording
of the Mac for these; the point is what the lens shows.

| # | Shows | Before | After | Setup and tips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blurry edges vs edge margin | A full-screen white window, Edge Margin Off. Move the phone to a corner to show the soft band. | Edge Margin 90%. The same corner now shows a black border and a sharp window edge. | Use a window with a thin dark title bar and a few text lines near the corner so the blur is obvious. |
| 2 | 60 vs 120 Hz scroll | Displays set to 60 Hz. Scroll a long page of text at a steady pace (trackpad, one flick). | 120 Hz, same page and flick. | Phone at 60 fps or higher; 120 fps slow motion if available. Use the same page; scroll once per clip. |
| 3 | Default vs HiDPI text | Default scaled resolution. A paragraph of 12 pt text, phone close to the lens. | A HiDPI 1920 by 1080 size, same paragraph. | Same text size on screen in both; only the rendering should differ. Film the text at the centre, not the edge. |
| 4 | White vs dark mode transparency | Light appearance, a bright window over the room. Show the room through the glasses. | Dark appearance; the dark window lets the room show through. | Same room, same lighting, same head position. Put a recognisable object behind the window. |
| 5 | Night Shift missing vs warm tint | Night Shift on in System Settings at maximum warmth; film the glasses (no change expected). | Lenses night tint on Warm, then Candle. | Show the Mac's own screen next to the glasses image first, so the viewer sees Night Shift warming the laptop only. |
| 6 | Small vs big pointer | Default pointer on a wide window. Move across the screen. | Pointer size slider near the right. | Move the pointer in the same path in both. |

Each clip needs a one-line caption in the edit (no price, no claims beyond what shows). Say "Tested on RayNeo Air" once at the end.
Label any pair that is a drawing, not footage, as an illustration.
