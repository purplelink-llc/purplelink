# RubbleFront trailer v2: storyboard

Replaces v1 (a tour of the interface with a narrator). v2 is a trailer: a cold open on a number, cuts on the beat of the game's own 120 bpm battle music, impact flashes, shake and 4 to 5 frame freezes on key hits, short text cards instead of narration, one drop at the title, then the run and the outpost, then a fast finale and one held shot.

Source: `storyboards/rubblefront-v2.json` (main), `rubblefront-v2-30s.json`, `rubblefront-v2-15s.json` (9:16). All three are generated by `video/gen-v2.py` from one shot library. Footage is captured by `game/tools/trailer_runner.gd` (branch `trailer-v2` in the Frontlink repo, clips named `c_*`), built by `video/build-rf2.mjs` (Remotion) and `video/mixrf2.py` (mix), written to `Frontlink/build/trailer/v2/`.

## Rhythm and music

- Music is only the game's own tracks: `battle_low`, `battle_mid`, `battle_high` and their `_drive` stems (all 120 bpm, D minor, 48 bars; same position in each so they swap on a bar line). Beat = 0.5 s, bar = 2 s. The music starts at t = 3 s so its downbeats fall on odd seconds; the first impact (t = 3.0), the card slams and the logo (t = 21.0) land on downbeats.
- 0 to 3 s: low track under a synthesised riser and a heartbeat tick; 3 to 20 s: mid plus drive; 20 to 21 s: music dips to silence, riser; 21 s: boom and the logo with high plus drive; 29 to 45 s: mid for the run and outpost; 45 s on: high plus drive for the finale; a half-second of silence before the last line, then a boom.
- Sound effects: the game's own effects recorded with each clip at normal speed, plus synthesised (sines and noise, nothing sampled) boom, hit, slam, whoosh, tick and riser. Mixed to about -14 LUFS integrated (loudnorm, true peak at most -2 dB).

## Text cards (all under 7 words, no narrator)

EVERY SHOT HAS ODDS. / BLOW A HOLE IN THEIR COVER. / HOLD. WAIT. AMBUSH. / BOARDS UP TO 18 BY 14. / PRAY THE 78% HOLDS. / SNOW. SAND. RAIN. NIGHT. / CHOOSE YOUR ROUTE. / PICK ONE OF THREE. / BUILD THE OUTPOST. / KEEP YOUR VETERANS ALIVE. / GO DEEPER. / A NEW BATTLE EVERY DAY. / ONE MORE RUN.

Dropped from the candidate list: "Pray the 95% holds." No staged shot reaches 95 percent (the best the staging reaches is 90), so the card uses the 78 percent that is on screen when the titan shot misses.
