What Haea does

Daily readiness briefing

A short morning briefing with your readiness score and one suggestion for the day. Tap it for the full plan: what is helping, what is holding you back, and what to avoid.

Strength training log

Log workouts with your own routines or proven programs. See weekly volume by muscle, personal records with estimated one-rep maxes, your strength level against population standards, and a deload check that watches your fatigue.

Running and route planning

Track runs on iPhone or Apple Watch, plan a route on the map and follow it, and see training load, weekly distance, personal records and time in each heart rate zone.

Insights

Pick an outcome (sleep, running pace, weight or mood) and see which of your habits move it most, how strong each link is, and how it has changed. Try a small experiment to test a change.

Share cards

Turn a metric into a clean card for your story or feed.

Reads Apple Health

Haea reads sleep, heart rate, HRV, workouts and weight from Apple Health. It does not log food: it reads the nutrition that apps such as MacroFactor or Cronometer write to Apple Health.

Haea Premium

Energy balance and TDEE

Estimates your total daily energy expenditure (TDEE, the calories you burn in a day) from your weight and the calories in Apple Health, and shows your balance over rolling windows.

Circadian rhythm

Shows how your energy and sleep change through the day, with your best windows for focus and for winding down.

Running analytics

FIT file export, route maps colored by pace, mean max power curves, and a polarized training zone breakdown.

Advanced models

Dose-response curves, Granger causality (a test of whether one measure helps predict another) and adaptation tracking show which changes in your data come before others.

Biological age and VO₂ max

An estimate of your biological age and your aerobic fitness, tracked over time.

Private by design

No account, no cloud sync, no ads and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Your records are kept in a database on your iPhone, and all analysis runs there. The coach uses Apple Intelligence on the device. See Haea and your health data.

Frequently asked questions

When does Haea ship?
Haea is expected to launch in 2026. Join the waitlist above to be notified when it is available.
Is there a free tier?
Yes. Haea is free to download, and the daily briefing, strength log, running and share cards work without a subscription. Premium ($1.99 a month, $14.99 a year, or a one-time $49.99 for lifetime access) adds the deeper analysis: energy balance, circadian rhythm, running analytics, advanced models, biological age and VO2 max, and widgets.
Does Haea sync to the cloud?
No. Haea has no account, no cloud sync, no ads and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Your records are stored and analyzed on your iPhone, and your health data is never sent to Purplelink or anyone else. If you plan a running route, Apple Maps calculates the path, and if you connect Oura with a personal access token, Haea fetches your Oura data directly from Oura to your phone.
What data does Haea use? Does it log food?
Haea reads from Apple Health: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), workouts, weight and more. Haea does not log food. It reads the nutrition that apps such as MacroFactor or Cronometer write to Apple Health. You can also connect an Oura ring.
Is Haea HIPAA-compliant?
Haea is a consumer app and not a covered entity under HIPAA (the US health privacy law for providers and insurers). All data is stored on your device with no cloud transmission or third-party access, so Purplelink holds no copy of your health records to breach.
