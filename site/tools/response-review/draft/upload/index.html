Upload your manuscripts and the reviewer comments

We draft a reply to every reviewer comment, check each stated change against your manuscripts, then run the Response Review panel on the draft. The result is a first draft for you to edit, not a letter to send as it is.

Verifying your payment…

If your payment did not go through to this page. Check the email titled "link to start" that we sent to the address you paid with. It opens this page on any device. Your payment is recorded if you have a Stripe receipt. If the link says it was already used, the draft is probably under way: open the status link from that same email. Still stuck? Email ben@purplelink.llc with your receipt.

Original manuscript (required, the version the reviewers read)

Drag the original manuscript PDF here, or choose one. Up to 20 MB.

Revised manuscript (optional, strongly recommended)

Drag the revised manuscript PDF here, or choose one. Up to 20 MB.

With the revised PDF, each reply that says you changed something is checked against it. Without it, every change in the draft is left as a placeholder for you to fill in.
