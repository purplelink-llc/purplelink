Your license keys

Preparing your keys…

Each app runs free for 7 days from the first time you open it. Then it asks for its key: paste it where the app asks (ModernTex: “Have a license key?”; Outbound Veil and Legroom: Enter license key). A key unlocks only its own app, for good, with no account. Keyfeel’s and Tapefolio’s keys are with their downloads below.

ModernTex

Preparing your download…

Drag ModernTex to Applications and open it. When it asks, choose “Have a license key?” and paste its key from above.

Outbound Veil

Preparing your download…

Drag it to Applications and open it. Paste its key from above when it asks. macOS asks for Accessibility permission on first launch.

Legroom

Preparing your download…

Drag it to Applications and open it. Paste its key from above when it asks. Legroom lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon.

Keyfeel

Preparing your download…

Open the disk image, drag Keyfeel to Applications, and open it. It runs free for 7 days; choose Enter License Key in its menu and paste the key above to keep it. macOS asks for the Input Monitoring permission on first launch; Keyfeel reads which key was pressed, never the characters you type.

Tapefolio

Preparing your download…

Open the disk image, drag Tapefolio to Applications, and open it. It needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. It runs free for 7 days; choose Enter License Key in its menu and paste the key above to keep it. The first time each model runs on a Mac, it can take a few minutes while macOS prepares it for the Neural Engine. Until you download a speech model, Tapefolio uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your files stay on your Mac.

Vitae and your Vitae Plus key

Download Vitae for Mac (free)

Preparing your key…

In Vitae, open Settings, then Vitae Plus, paste the key and click Activate. This key is for life: it never renews and nothing will be charged. Older copies of Vitae may show a price next to the plan; ignore it.
