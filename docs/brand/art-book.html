Two · colour

Hue 310, from the site

The mark is a vertical gradient between two purples from the site's palette. The wordmark's text is never purple: it takes the ink of whatever it sits on. Its underline swoosh and star carry the same purple gradient as the mark, left to right.

Three · construction

How the lockups are built

  1. Wordmark width is 72 percent of the mark's width, centred beneath it.
  2. Gap between mark and wordmark is a quarter of the wordmark's height.
  3. Clear space on every side equals the height of the "software company" line.
  4. Side-by-side version: the mark sits left of wordmark B at the same height, 2175 x 448. Use it where a stacked logo would be too tall: headers, footers, About windows, boot splashes.
  5. Minimum size: 160 px wide on screen or 30 mm in print. Smaller than that, use the mark (at least 16 px).
  6. The wordmark is Ben's artwork, never retyped in a site font. It reads "purplelink / software company".
  7. The meaning: a hyperlink turns purple once you have clicked it. The swoosh is the link's underline and the star is the click.
Four · where each one goes

Use by surface

The rule underneath the table: the striped mark always accompanies wordmark B. Stacked (mark above) in large and hero spots, side by side (mark left) in small and signature spots. The mark alone stays for small square uses: favicon, avatars, the site footer icon.

Seven · design log

Decisions, in order

  1. 2026-10-28

    Mark adopted

    Eight versions of a striped P-L monogram arrived. The mark was traced to a vector from the image's transparency, so its edges stay sharp at any size, and filled with a hue-310 gradient. The gradient was tuned twice until it read on both themes.

  2. 2026-10-28

    Same file names

    The new mark replaced the old files in place, so all 389 pages picked it up without markup edits. All 94 per-page social cards were re-rendered.

  3. 2026-10-29

    Primary logo is the lockup

    “The main one should be the purplelink softwares one underneath the new logo.” The wordmark sits centred beneath the mark at 72 percent of its width.

  4. 2026-10-29

    Wordmark alone inside products (reversed 2026-10-30)

    Circled Hoops should carry just “purplelink softwares” at the bottom. Generalised to every app, game and product video.

  5. 2026-10-29

    Logo files are versioned

    Browsers kept the old mark for up to 30 days. Every logo reference now carries a content hash, so a new logo reaches everyone on their next page load.

  6. 2026-10-30

    Wordmark B: "software company"

    The wordmark now reads "purplelink / software company", with the underline swoosh and star in the brand purple gradient. A hyperlink turns purple once you have clicked it; the swoosh is the link's underline and the star is the click. The site header shows it beside the mark. Forward only: published videos, cards and posts keep the old wordmark.

  7. 2026-10-30

    The mark comes back with the wordmark

    “I still want the <> P logo that we had before above it or to the side of it where relevant.” (Ben.) This reverses the 2026-10-29 entry “Wordmark alone inside products”, which stays above as history. The striped mark now always accompanies wordmark B: stacked in large and hero spots (press, covers, credits end, cards), side by side with the mark on the left in small and signature spots (site header, site and game footers, app About windows, game boot splash). The mark alone stays for favicon, avatars and the footer icon. New files: purplelink-lockup-horizontal-dark / -light.
