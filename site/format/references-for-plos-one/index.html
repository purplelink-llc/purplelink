PLOS ONE reference format

PLOS ONE uses Vancouver style: references numbered in the order they appear, cited in square brackets, with the first six authors and then “et al.” The PLOS LaTeX template ships plos2025.bst.

Checked 2026-10-01 against the sources linked at the end of this page.

What PLOS ONE expects

  • Reference style: The ICMJE style, also called Vancouver. Numbered citation-sequence method, with the number in square brackets in the text, e.g. [19].
  • Authors: First six authors, then et al.
  • BibTeX style: plos2025.bst, in the PLOS LaTeX template zip.
  • Journal names: Abbreviated as in the NCBI databases.
  • DOI form: Written as doi: 10.1016/j.molimm.2014.11.005 at the end of the reference. Do not use a shortened DOI or the URL.
  • LaTeX submissions: Submitted as PDF. A manuscript that does not follow the template format may be sent back.

Setting up the bibliography

From the PLOS LaTeX template, which uses the standard article class:

\documentclass[10pt,letterpaper]{article}
...
\bibliographystyle{plos2025}
...
\bibliography{plos_bibtex_sample}

Replace plos_bibtex_sample with the name of your .bib file.

LaTeX template

PLOS provides one template for all its journals on the PLOS ONE LaTeX page: a zip with plos_latex_template.tex, plos2025.bst and a sample .bib file. It also works in Overleaf. We do not have a PLOS template page yet.

Common mistakes

  • Listing every author. PLOS style stops at six and adds et al.
  • Pasting the DOI as an https://doi.org/ link in a hand-typed list. The guidelines ask for the doi: label and the full DOI, and say not to give a shortened DOI or the URL.
  • Citations in the abstract. The guidelines say not to include them.
  • Spelled-out journal names. The guidelines ask for NCBI abbreviations, and the .bst prints the journal field as you typed it.
  • Citing unpublished work in the reference list, such as manuscripts submitted but not yet accepted.

Frequently asked

What reference style does PLOS ONE use?

Vancouver, the ICMJE style: numbered in order of appearance, cited in square brackets, with the first six authors followed by et al.

Is there a PLOS ONE .bst file?

Yes. The PLOS LaTeX template zip contains plos2025.bst, and PLOS says to use it so references are generated in the correct format.

Does PLOS ONE have a word limit?

The submission guidelines say the main sections have no word limit, and ask that the language be clear and concise.
