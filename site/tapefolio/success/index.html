Preparing your key…

In Tapefolio, choose Enter License Key and paste it. It unlocks Tapefolio for good on up to two of your Macs, with no account, and works offline. It is also in your receipt email.

Installing

Open the disk image

Drag Tapefolio to Applications, then open it from there. It needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. It runs free for 7 days until you enter the key.

Expect a wait the first time

The first time each model runs on a Mac, it can take a few minutes while macOS prepares it for the Neural Engine. It happens once for each model. Until you download a speech model, Tapefolio uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, and macOS may fetch Apple's own model once.

Better models are optional

Parakeet, Whisper base, Whisper large-v3 turbo and the Nemotron 3 speaker-separation model are downloads in Settings, from Hugging Face, each once. Settings can delete them again. Your files never leave your Mac.

It updates itself

The app checks for new versions and offers to install them in place. You can also choose Check for Updates from its menu.
