South coast waterfalls

The road along the south coast passes one waterfall after another. Seljalandsfoss you can walk behind; Gljúfrabúi hides in a cleft a few hundred metres on.

Seljalandsfoss falls about 60 metres and a path runs behind the curtain of water.
