Update

Vitae 0.4: start from your CV

CV import now reads papers, grants, degrees, positions, honors, talks, teaching, service and mentoring from Word, PDF and other CV files, in the common citation styles, and shows you everything before it is saved; anything already in your library is flagged and left out. CV formats: seven ready-made formats (full academic CV, short, one page, industry résumé, APA, IEEE, teaching-focused) plus your own, and Export Several Formats writes them all at once. Send to Zotero adds your publications to your Zotero library after showing every change, and never removes anything. Feedback: Help > Send Feedback or the envelope in the sidebar opens an email to the developer. Delivered through the app's own update check.

September 23, 2026
Update

Vitae 0.3.1: an acceptance-rate directory

Acceptance Rates (Venues toolbar): search about 13,700 venue acceptance rates by name or abbreviation. Record a rate you found, with its year, counts and source, and Vitae keeps it in your library and uses it ahead of the list wherever it suggests an acceptance probability. Check for Newer Rates updates the list, only when you ask. Verified journal senders: an email counts as coming from a journal's editorial system only when your mail provider confirmed its signature. Also fixes for mail servers, backups and merging, VoiceOver labels on every icon-only button, and plainer wording. Delivered through the app's own update check.

September 23, 2026
Update

Vitae 0.3: the Inbox reads your mail

Mail. Connect iCloud, Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Fastmail or a university mailbox in Settings > Email & AI. Vitae checks for new mail when it opens and every 15 minutes, keeps only decision letters, deadlines, review invitations and submission receipts, and puts each one in the Inbox for review. Applying a decision saves the letter with that review round. Messages stay unread, and app passwords stay in the Mac's Keychain. Backups and undo. A snapshot of the library every day Vitae opens and before any bulk change, restorable from Settings > Backups, and Edit > Undo for every change, including deletes. Also new: target venues and checklists per submission, manuscript versions, a revision workspace for reviewer comments, Library Health with one-click lookup of every missing DOI, Shareables for LinkedIn, Instagram and X, and in-app updates from this version on. Vitae Plus is an optional subscription, $3 a month or $24 a year with a 7-day free trial, for reports, Word templates and a few extras. Everything else stays free. Version 0.1 cannot update itself, so download 0.3 once from the Vitae page.

September 13, 2026
Update

ModernTex 1.3.0: a real Table Editor

Table Editor (Compile menu, the toolbar's Table Editor button, or ⌘⇧T): paste a range copied from Excel or Google Sheets and it lands in the right cells, growing the table only if the pasted data is bigger than what's there — never smaller. Drag a column's edge to resize it; press Return in a cell for a real line break, which turns that column into a wrapping paragraph column automatically. Excel-native keys throughout: Return confirms a cell and moves down a row, ⌥Return is Excel's own line-break chord, arrow keys move between rows, and a smart ⌘V detects a real pasted range versus a single value. Set a table-wide font size and family for a dense results table. Pick a table already in your document from the Tables menu and edit it in place, or start from a preset. Read more about it, and the TikZ Designer from the last release, on the blog. Delivered through the app's own update check.

September 12, 2026
Update

ModernTex 1.2.0: a visual TikZ workflow

TikZ Designer (Compile menu, the toolbar's TikZ Figure Editor button, or ⌘⌥⇧K): build a diagram by dragging boxes and arrows instead of writing TikZ by hand — real colors, resizable boxes, drag-to-connect with a live preview line, double-click to rename, undo, duplicate, a dashed Note box for callouts, and presets to start from (flowchart, pipeline, state machine, tree). Pick a figure already in your document from the Figures menu and edit it directly, or insert a new one — either way it drops in plain, readable TikZ. New and still evolving, so double-check the generated code. TikZ Preview (⌘⌥K) renders just the tikzpicture under your cursor on its own, with instant warnings for a missing semicolon or an unclosed brace before you run a full compile. Also in this release: a proper in-app User Guide, and the license keys described below. Delivered through the app's own update check.

ModernTex 1.1.0

Formatting shortcuts (⌘B/⌘I/⌘U/⌘⇧C, compile moved to ⌘↵); sync between your Macs or with a co-author in two words, Get Changes and Send Changes; Compile Only This Chapter and Draft Mode for large documents; references resolve in Live and Fast modes too; Import Reviewed Word File brings a supervisor's tracked changes and comments into the source; math typing shortcuts (1/x, dint, ->, x1) with tab-stop snippets; Fix with AI proposes a repair for a build error (Apple Intelligence on your Mac, or your own Claude/OpenAI account) and shows it as a diff before applying; PDF and source selection follow each other both ways; and XeLaTeX/LuaLaTeX are now auto-detected from a project's own % !TEX program = … line, the same directive TeXShop, TeXstudio, and VS Code's LaTeX Workshop read — no more silent pdflatex on a fontspec/polyglossia document. Delivered through the app's own update check.
